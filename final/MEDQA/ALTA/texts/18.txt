A man in his fifties is brought to the emergency department because of worsening shortness of breath. Recently, he has had intermittent chest pain that worsens with certain positions and actions. He has also had symptoms consistent with a respiratory infection for the past 2 weeks. He has a history of cardiovascular risk factors and takes medications for these conditions. He appears pale and sweaty. His temperature is elevated, and his pulse and respirations are increased. Blood pressure shows variability between expiration and inspiration. Examination shows signs of potential heart-related complications.
