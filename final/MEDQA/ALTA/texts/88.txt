An adult male is brought to the emergency department for ongoing vomiting that began several hours ago. He describes the vomitus as discolored. He consumes alcohol daily and has not seen a physician regularly in years. He appears unwell. Vital signs include an elevated pulse and low blood pressure. Physical examination shows weak pulses, slow capillary refill, several skin findings, and a swollen abdomen. He is oriented to person but not fully aware of his surroundings.
