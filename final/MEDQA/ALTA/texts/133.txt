A woman nearing full-term pregnancy presents to the hospital complaining of contractions consistent with latent labor. She reports not experiencing any significant symptoms like discharge or bleeding and is currently not on any medications. On examination, her vital signs are stable. She has had minimal prenatal care and has a history of inconsistent contraception use. Her sexually transmitted infections status is unknown. During the medical evaluation, she undergoes a series of tests, resulting in the appropriate administration of medication during delivery. The infant receives medication as a preventive measure. A confirmatory test is then performed to diagnose a condition in the mother.
