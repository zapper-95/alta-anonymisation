An individual presented to the hospital due to breathing difficulties. There is a history of a chronic condition but often does not follow the recommended treatment regimen. The patient reports experiencing difficulty breathing when lying down, nighttime breathing problems, and general tiredness. On physical examination, the blood pressure is low. There is neck vein distention. An abnormal heart sound, lung crackles, and noticeable swelling of the legs were also detected. An imaging test was performed, which showed reduced heart function.
