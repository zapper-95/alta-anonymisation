A patient presents with nasal secretions and breathing difficulties. They report weight loss over the last two months with no changes in diet. They have also been suffering from joint pain for some time. Their past medical history is unremarkable. Vital signs are normal. Physical examination is significant for nasal symptoms. Respiratory examination notes abnormal lung sounds. Laboratory findings suggest inflammation. The patient is prescribed medication but returns after some time without any clinical improvement, indicating the issue may not be infectious.
