A person with a chronic lung condition presents to the emergency department with a cough and breathing difficulty, indicating a potential exacerbation of their condition. Regular medications include inhaled treatments. The person has made lifestyle changes in an attempt to manage the progression of their lung disease. Temperature, pulse rate, and blood pressure are within the normal range, and respirations are elevated. On physical examination, the use of additional muscles during breathing is evident. Auscultation of the chest reveals abnormal breath sounds. Oxygen saturation is noted to be low, suggesting inadequate oxygenation related to the underlying condition.
