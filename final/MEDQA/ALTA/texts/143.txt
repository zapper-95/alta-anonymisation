A person presents to the emergency department with difficulty breathing after exposure to cold air during an outdoor activity. The patient informs the physician of a history of respiratory issues but mentions occasional use of related medication. The patient is not a regular smoker. On physical examination, vital signs are abnormal. Auscultation of the chest reveals wheezing. The physician first offers a type of medication typically used for respiratory conditions, but the patient declines. The physician then prescribes an alternative treatment, which results in clinical improvement.
