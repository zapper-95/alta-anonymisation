An individual is brought to a medical facility accompanied by a close associate due to changes in behavior. The associate notes there has been less interaction with others recently. The individual had previously been more socially active. The individual experiences beliefs of external monitoring and thought influence. They do not consume alcohol and had stopped using certain substances prior. Standard checks show typical results. Physical assessment reveals no irregularities. During the mental status examination, their thought process is disorganized. They seem tense and have reduced emotional expression.
