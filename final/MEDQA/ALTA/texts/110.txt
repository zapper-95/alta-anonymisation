An adult female presents with a neck enlargement which made swallowing difficult for some time. She has had digestive issues recently and overall fatigue. She also had menstrual irregularities; and often feels cold at home. On the other hand, she has controlled respiratory issues and allergies. On examination, the thyroid is abnormal and enlarged. Laboratory studies indicate abnormal thyroid function. A thyroid scan indicates that the nodule has reduced iodine uptake.
