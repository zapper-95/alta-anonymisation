A middle-aged woman comes to the emergency department because of a short history of abdominal pain and nausea. She is of average height and has a high BMI. She has a fever. Physical examination shows a distended abdomen and tenderness with normal bowel sounds. Laboratory studies show an elevated leukocyte count and normal liver enzymes. Abdominal ultrasonography is performed, but the results are not definitive. Additional imaging is performed to evaluate bile ducts and other abdominal structures.
