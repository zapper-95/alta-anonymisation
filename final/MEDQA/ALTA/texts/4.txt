A newborn is brought to the physician because of increasing yellowing of the eyes and skin. The parents have noticed that the stools have become pale. The newborn was switched to formula feeds recently after reading online that breastfeeding could be the cause of the current symptoms. The patient was delivered at term. Pregnancy and delivery were uncomplicated. The newborn appears healthy. Vital signs are normal. Examination shows jaundice. The liver is enlarged. Cardiopulmonary examination shows no abnormalities. Neurologic examination shows no focal findings. Serum studies show elevated bilirubin and liver enzyme levels.
