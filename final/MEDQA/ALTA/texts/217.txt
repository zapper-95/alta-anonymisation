A patient is brought to the emergency department because of a history of fever, chills, and weakness. He has no cough. He was treated for an infection some time ago. He takes no medications. He has a history of smoking and alcohol use. He has a history of substance use. He appears ill. Examination shows skin marks. There are eye changes. The lungs are normal to auscultation. A heart murmur is heard. Laboratory studies show elevated white blood cell count. Echocardiography shows valve issues. Blood cultures are collected.
