A middle-aged man is brought to the hospital with respiratory symptoms like dry cough and shortness of breath, along with a low-grade fever that started several days ago. He also reports experiencing gastrointestinal issues such as diarrhea in recent days. His temperature is recorded as 38.0°C (100.4°F). Diffuse crackles are heard over both lung fields. Laboratory studies indicate an increased leukocyte count, suggestive of infection, and specific electrolyte imbalances including low sodium levels. An imaging study of the chest reveals patchy abnormalities in both lungs.
