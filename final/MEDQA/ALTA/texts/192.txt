A patient near delivery is admitted to the labor and delivery unit complaining of frequent contractions. Previous births have included surgical procedures, but the patient wishes to attempt a different type of delivery this time. Prenatal care is notable for a condition controlled with lifestyle changes. The delivery is prolonged, but the patient's pain is controlled with medication. After delivery, fundal massage is performed, but the placenta does not pass. The medical team manually removes the placenta, and the patient experiences significant blood loss, during which blood pressure decreases.
