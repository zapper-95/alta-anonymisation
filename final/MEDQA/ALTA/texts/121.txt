A patient presents to their doctor with complaints of feeling unwell, tiredness, a sore throat, and elevated body temperature over the last several days. Vital signs are recorded and found to be mostly within normal ranges. Physical examination shows enlargement of an organ in the abdominal area, throat irritation, and swollen lymph nodes. Laboratory work-up shows an increased number of a type of white blood cell and atypical cells in the blood. A specific blood test also returns a positive result.
