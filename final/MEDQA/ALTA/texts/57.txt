An infant is brought to the pediatrician by their parents. They are concerned that the child becomes lethargic and irritated between meals. They found that feeding more often with small servings helps. The child was born full-term via natural delivery and is meeting developmental milestones. The mother has a sibling that occasionally requires blood transfusions. The infant's vital signs show normal temperature, and the child appears irritated and slightly jaundiced on exam. The heart has a regular rate and rhythm and the lungs are clear. Blood work shows signs consistent with anemia, and certain hemoglobinopathies are ruled out. A Coombs test is negative. The physician determined that the findings are related to an enzyme deficiency.
