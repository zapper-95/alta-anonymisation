A young adult presents to the emergency department with a chief complaint related to urinary issues. She states that this has been one of many symptoms she has experienced lately. At times she has had trouble speaking and has noticed changes in her vision, however these episodes occurred over a month ago and have resolved since then. Recently she experienced severe facial pain that was exacerbated by certain actions. The patient has no significant past medical history. Her current medications include a common over-the-counter medication. On physical exam it is noted that there is an unusual eye movement pattern. The patient's initial workup is started in the emergency department. Her vital signs are within normal limits and you note someone who appears to need further care.
