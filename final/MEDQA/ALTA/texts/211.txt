An adult undergoes surgery for a fractured femur sustained after an accident. A few days after the procedure, symptoms such as a high fever and redness develop around the surgical site. The patient's temperature reads significantly above normal. Physical examination shows purulent discharge from the wound with surrounding skin redness. Wound culture identifies gram-positive bacteria. Treatment with an antibiotic is started. Over the next few days, the patient continues to have a high-grade fever, pain, and purulent discharge.
