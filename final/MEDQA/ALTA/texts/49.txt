A middle-aged adult is brought to the clinic by a relative for evaluation. The relative reports that the patient has recently been having difficulty with daily grooming activities. The patient’s family history includes cardiovascular issues. The patient’s past medical history is positive for gastrointestinal conditions. No medication use is reported. Vital signs are within normal limits. On physical examination, the patient’s neck is tense and there is shoulder discomfort; muscle strength is intact. Laboratory work is performed and presented below: Hemoglobin level is within normal range. Hematocrit and leukocyte count are unremarkable. Differential counts show typical ranges. Mean corpuscular volume is observed. Platelet count is standard. The erythrocyte sedimentation rate is elevated. C-reactive protein is increased.
