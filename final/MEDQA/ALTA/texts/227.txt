A patient comes to the physician because of a fever, persistent headache, nausea, and general discomfort for a while. During this time, they have also noticed gum bleeding and scattered marks on the body that may suggest a bleeding issue. They recently returned from travel. Various vital signs are elevated. Examination shows pale complexion and mild yellowing of the eyes. Physical and neurologic examinations show no other abnormalities. Laboratory studies show low hemoglobin and platelet levels, suggesting possible anemia and thrombocytopenia. Blood cultures are negative.
