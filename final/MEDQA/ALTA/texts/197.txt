An infant is brought to the physician by their parent because of a history of recurrent nonbilious vomiting. Vomiting occurs almost immediately after most feeds. Each time, they become irritable while feeding and refuse to finish the bottle. The infant has a condition associated with certain genetic markers. Their vaccinations are up-to-date. They appear agitated and cry during the examination. The temperature is 37.2°C, pulse is high, respirations are rapid, and blood pressure is measured. Examination shows features consistent with this genetic condition. The abdomen is soft and nontender. An epigastric mass is palpated. Cardiac examination shows no abnormalities. Arterial blood gas analysis is within normal limits.
