A middle-aged person presents to a healthcare provider with abdominal pain. They say that the pain "comes and goes" throughout the day and usually lasts for some time per episode. The pain is localized in the upper abdomen. They deny any feeling of regurgitation or nighttime cough but mention experiencing digestive discomfort. They report that eating smaller, more frequent meals improves the pain. They have noticed a slight weight change over time. They deny any significant change in their gastrointestinal patterns. They consume alcohol socially and have a history of smoking. They deny any family history of cancer. On physical exam, they are tender in the abdomen. Various diagnostic procedures are undertaken to identify the condition.
