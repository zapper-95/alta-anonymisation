A middle-aged person presents with complaints of difficulty breathing and a cough. They report struggling to get comfortable at night to sleep. The patient has not seen a healthcare provider in many years. They have a history of alcohol use. Family history is non-significant, and the patient does not take any medications. Their temperature is within normal range, blood pressure is high, pulse is rapid, and respiratory rate is high. Physical examination reveals fluid retention in the legs. Chest auscultation reveals abnormal lung sounds.
