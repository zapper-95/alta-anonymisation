A young adult is brought to a medical facility due to a history of foot pain. The individual has been engaging in physical activities, including running several kilometers daily. Initially, the pain only occurred during activity, but now it is also present when not active. The pain is generally located over the forefoot. Vital signs are normal. Examination shows mild swelling over part of the forefoot. Pressing the forefoot causes pain. The rest of the examination shows normal findings. An x-ray of the foot shows slight loss of cortical density at the forefoot consistent with a stress reaction.
