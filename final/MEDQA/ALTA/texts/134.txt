A group of researchers conducted a study that included patients from a broader region who had a medical diagnosis and interactions with healthcare services over a period. Mortality was studied in relation to various factors. Mortality ratios were calculated, with overall mortality being higher when compared with studies that looked at inpatient individuals. Higher mortality rates were observed in some groups, particularly soon after registration. Increased mortality was also found in patients with certain diagnoses. It was concluded that the overall mortality of psychiatric patients managed outside of institutional settings was higher than expected; however, it was still lower than the mortality described in inpatient psychiatric settings.
