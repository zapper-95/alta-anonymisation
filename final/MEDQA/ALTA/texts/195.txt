A person presents to the clinic with abdominal pain that has been worsening for the last several days. They say that they often get similar pain but less severe and that it worsens with meals. The pain sometimes spreads to other areas. They recently lost weight due to a lack of appetite since their last visit. They admit to alcohol consumption. Their temperature is within normal range, respirations are typical, pulse is average, and blood pressure is stable. Physical examination reveals abdominal tenderness. An abdominal CT scan shows changes.
