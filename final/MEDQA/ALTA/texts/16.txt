A person is brought into the emergency department by authorities. They state that the individual has been detained several times, but has recently undergone a significant change in their living situation. On exam, the patient is agitated. Their vitals are all within normal limits. They appear disoriented and have difficulty speaking clearly. On gait exam, the patient struggles to maintain balance. Labs return with the following values: Na and K within normal range, and a slightly elevated Cr. His ABG shows a lower pH. Blood salicylate levels are not elevated.
