A middle-aged person complains of chest pain that has been present for several hours during a hospitalization for a heart attack. Recently, they had a burning sensation in their chest and discomfort in their shoulder. Upon initial presentation to the hospital’s emergency department, they were diagnosed with a heart attack and treated. They have been under observation since then. The patient now describes discomfort in their chest and shoulder, similar to what initially brought them into the hospital. A pulse, respiratory rate, and a blood pressure are observed. Auscultation of the chest reveals no abnormal findings. The patient's ECG is difficult to interpret.
