A person is brought to their primary care physician by their partner who is concerned about the frequent difficulty walking. Several months ago, their partner started noticing changes in their walking speed. They have experienced issues with balance multiple times in the past weeks, and there is concern that they will sustain a serious injury if this continues. The patient is retired and was physically active until recently. They do not smoke. Their family history includes neurological conditions. Their vital signs are normal. On exam, their movements appear slowed. They shuffle when they walk. Muscle tone is increased in their extremities.
