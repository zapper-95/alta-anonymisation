A man presents to a walk-in clinic with fever, chills, night sweats, anorexia, malaise, progressive shortness of breath, and sharp chest pain. He is found with an average blood pressure, an average heart rate, an elevated respiratory rate, and a heart murmur. His medical history includes multiple healthcare visits for gastrointestinal issues. He denies the use of psychoactive drugs. A chest plain film shows clear lungs and changes in the heart silhouette, and echocardiography reports abnormalities involving the heart.
