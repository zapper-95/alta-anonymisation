An adult presents to the clinic with complaints of fever, loss of appetite, fatigue, and a persistent cough. The symptoms include a subtle fever and occasional blood-tinged sputum. The patient has significant weight loss and experiences severe night sweats. A plain radiograph of the patient’s chest shows a pattern indicating a lung issue. Baseline investigations show the following: Complete blood count, Hemoglobin level below the normal range, White blood cell count elevated, Differential count showing an increased lymphocyte percentage, and elevated eosinophils, Erythrocyte sedimentation rate elevated. The physician suspects that the patient may be experiencing a respiratory condition.
