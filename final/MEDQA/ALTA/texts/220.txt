A middle-aged woman comes to the physician for a follow-up examination. Her last Pap smear showed an abnormal result. On questioning, she has had fatigue and an increase in abdominal girth despite weight loss over the past several months. She has a chronic digestive condition and a thyroid condition. Her last menstrual period was a few years ago. Current medications include medication for digestive issues and thyroid issues. Abdominal examination shows shifting dullness. There is tenderness to palpation of the lower abdomen but no guarding or rebound. Bimanual palpation shows an abdominal mass.
