A patient in their sixties presents to the emergency department because of severe leg pain. The condition started suddenly. He has a medical history of hypertension for which he takes medication. He does not smoke or use substances. His temperature is normal, the blood pressure is within the normal range, and the pulse is elevated and irregular. On physical examination, the patient appears in severe pain and one leg is cooler than the other. The popliteal pulse is weaker on one side compared to the other.
