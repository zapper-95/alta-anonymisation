An elderly male patient presents to the emergency department with sudden difficulty breathing and a cough. The patient states that his symptoms came on while he was sleeping. The patient has a past medical history of chronic conditions and is managed with medications typical for such illnesses. On physical exam, you note swelling and signs that could suggest a cardiovascular issue. Imaging studies are obtained. The patient receives medical therapy, including respiratory support, and shows rapid improvement. Lab values indicate abnormal levels in some areas: Na+ is within normal range, K+ is outside normal range, Cl- is normal, HCO3- is elevated, BUN is normal, Glucose is normal, Creatinine is normal, and Ca2+ is outside normal range.
