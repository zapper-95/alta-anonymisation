An elderly man with a history of hypertension and heart rhythm issues comes to the physician for shortness of breath with mild exertion, progressive weakness, and a cough that has persisted for several months. He has been a long-term smoker. His medications include blood thinners and drugs for heart conditions. His pulse is irregular. Physical examination shows changes to the fingertips. A CT scan of the chest shows abnormalities in the lower parts of the lung.
