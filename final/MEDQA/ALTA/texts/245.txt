An adult is brought to the emergency department because of recent symptoms including fever and abdominal pain. Their partner reports that they have had difficulty recalling some information. They have received medical treatment multiple times recently. There is no known history of major illness within the family. They take minimal medication. They moved to the region a while ago. They appear unwell. Their temperature is elevated, pulse is slightly raised, and blood pressure is low. They are disoriented. Examination shows jaundice and skin lesions. There are slight tremors of the hands. The abdomen is enlarged with signs of fluid buildup. There is general tenderness to palpation with no guarding. Bowel sounds are decreased. Laboratory studies show: Hemoglobin level in normal range, Leukocyte count elevated, Platelet count within normal range, Serum Albumin low, Total bilirubin increased, Alkaline phosphatase normal, AST normal, ALT normal. Fluid analysis from the abdomen shows low albumin concentration and an elevated cell count with a high percentage of neutrophils.
