A man comes to the emergency department because of severe chest pain. He has coronary artery disease, hypertension, and type 2 diabetes. His current medications include aspirin, medications for cholesterol and blood pressure, and metformin. He has smoked for many years. On arrival, his pulse is irregular, and his blood pressure is low. The lungs are evaluated with no significant findings. Cardiac examination shows no abnormalities. An ECG is shown.
