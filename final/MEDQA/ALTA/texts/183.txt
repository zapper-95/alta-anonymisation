An unresponsive older adult is brought to the emergency department by ambulance and is quickly followed by a relative. Some time ago, they had surgery and various treatments for a serious condition and were discharged from the hospital with a medical device and a plan for further treatment. They were visiting family. According to the relative, their mental status was altered and health began to decline. The relative is not aware of their past medical status or medications. At the hospital, the person appears unwell and tired. Their heart rate is elevated with a regular rhythm. The area around their medical device is showing signs of irritation. The patient is admitted to the hospital, stabilized and started on treatment. The medical device is not functioning as expected. The treatment team concludes this patient has a health issue requiring attention.
