An individual presents to their primary care provider with several days of fever, malaise, breathing difficulty, productive cough, and has recently developed pleuritic chest pain. Their medical history includes a respiratory condition managed with treatment as needed, and they consume alcohol socially. Abnormal lung sounds are noted, and a chest radiograph shows focal consolidation. The person has a significant family history of serious lung conditions. After starting treatment, they join a study analyzing their immune response to lung infections. A protein in their serum is elevated due to the condition, and its interactions with proteins from their immune cells are studied.
