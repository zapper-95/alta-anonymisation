An individual presents to the clinic with discomfort and swelling of their legs, noted to have started several weeks ago. Initially minor, the swelling has progressed to the extent of making movement difficult. The individual has a history of a chronic medical condition. They are on regular medication for this condition. Vital signs show mild fever and elevated blood pressure. Urinalysis indicates abnormal results. The patient is prescribed a medication affecting the immune system. A few days later, they report urinary symptoms. Further examination reveals signs of bladder irritation.
