A child is brought to a medical facility with a prolonged history of fever and sore throat that worsened recently. She has difficulty swallowing and opening her mouth due to pain. There is a noticeable change in the quality of her voice. Her temperature is elevated, pulse is within normal range, respirations are normal, and blood pressure is stable. Examination shows neck swelling. Oropharyngeal examination shows redness and swelling in the throat. Laboratory studies are conducted to further assess her condition.
