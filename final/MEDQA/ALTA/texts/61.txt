A patient with a longstanding history of common cardiovascular conditions presents with abdominal discomfort and skin changes on the lower extremities. The symptoms began about a week ago and have worsened over time. The abdominal discomfort is mild to moderate and dull. Past medical history includes previous cardiovascular events. Medications include treatments for specific conditions as needed. The patient reports a history of tobacco and alcohol use. Review of systems shows decreased appetite. Vital signs are stable. On examination, the patient is alert and oriented. Heart and lung exams are unremarkable. A neck bruit is noted. The abdomen is unremarkable, except for the detection of a mass. Imaging reveals an abdominal aortic anomaly. Oxygen and fluid therapy are initiated. Medications for pain and blood pressure management are given.
