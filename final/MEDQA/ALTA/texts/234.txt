An individual arrives at the emergency department with nausea, abdominal discomfort that worsens after meals, and diarrhea for the last 2 days. They have been experiencing progressive perioral numbness and muscle cramping in the upper extremities for the past 24 hours. Some months prior, they underwent surgery for weight management, and there was a recent neck surgery. There is a history of chronic smoking. The only medication taken is a multivitamin supplement. The patient presents with fatigue. Vital signs include a regular temperature, heart rate, breathing rate, and normal blood pressure. A nurse notes a spasm in the hand during blood pressure measurement. Physical examination indicates a recently healed surgical wound on the neck and mild tenderness in the abdomen. Laboratory studies indicate that parathyroid hormone levels are notably elevated, suggesting an issue with calcium regulation.
