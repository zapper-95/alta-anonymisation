An individual is brought to the emergency department with a history of digestive issues but denies gastrointestinal bleeding or abdominal pain. Family has noticed some cognitive changes. History is significant for liver disease. Vital signs show a slightly elevated heart rate, normal temperature, and stable blood pressure. On examination, they appear to have some skin changes and reduced energy levels, are oriented to person and place but not time, and have abdominal distension. Neurological examination reveals movement irregularities, and the digestive tract issue is confirmed. Liver function tests show abnormal protein and enzyme levels.
