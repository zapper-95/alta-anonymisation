A young adult with a respiratory condition visits a physician for worsening shortness of breath. The patient states that their lung function has been steadily deteriorating. They further state that they have visited several other physicians who prescribed different treatments, but they feel these have not helped control the issues. The patient has experienced frequent respiratory infections in recent years and often suffers from unpredictable bouts of excessive coughing and wheezing. When asked if the coughing episodes produce sputum, they state that the sputum is discolored. They also state that the coughing and wheezing episodes are associated with fever and malaise. The vital signs include an elevated respiratory rate and normal temperature. On physical exam, the patient appears in moderate respiratory distress. Auscultation reveals abnormal breath sounds in the lung areas. Chest radiograph reveals various lung issues. Various lab tests show elevated markers.
