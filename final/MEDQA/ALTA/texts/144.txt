An infant is brought to the office by family due to sudden nose bleeding. The infant has a history of bruising and red spots that appeared without any trauma. There is no relevant history of bleeding issues. Vital signs show elevated heart and respiratory rates with normal blood pressure and temperature. The physical exam reveals pale skin color, spots in various areas as well as bruising. Blood count results show low Hemoglobin, increased Neutrophils, and normal Platelet count. Coagulation tests show elevated Fibrinogen. The blood smear indicates anemia-related changes and irregular platelet shapes. The bleeding time is longer than usual.
