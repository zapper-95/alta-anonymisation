A man presents to his primary care provider complaining of abdominal pain and constipation. He reports a history of steady abdominal pain. He has experienced reduced bowel movement frequency recently. His medical history includes hypertension and hyperlipidemia. He takes medication for his conditions. He has a long history of smoking and drinks alcohol regularly. His diet includes fast food. His temperature is elevated, blood pressure is high, pulse is normal, and respirations are normal. A review of the patient’s medical record reveals previous colonoscopy results. Relevant findings included multiple polyps which were removed, colonic mucosal outpouchings, and generally healthful findings.
