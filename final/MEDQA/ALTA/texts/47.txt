A middle-aged person presents to the emergency room with a recent onset of visual disturbance. They state that they were at home when they noticed the issue. Currently, they are also experiencing visual difficulties. Earlier, they felt ill with gastrointestinal symptoms, which they attributed to food they had eaten the day before. They mention someone who was with them has developed similar symptoms and was seen at another medical facility. While in the emergency room, their vital signs are within normal limits. An eye exam shows some abnormalities. The remainder of the exam is normal.
