A middle-aged individual presents with sudden-onset dyspnea. The past medical history includes circulatory issues in the lower extremities, a chronic condition that is managed with medication, and a history of tobacco use. The patient takes medications for their chronic condition. Family history includes a sibling who died from a vascular event. The temperature is slightly elevated, the pulse is elevated, the respirations are normal, and the blood pressure is normal. On physical examination, the patient shows signs of distress. Cardiac exam reveals an abnormal heart sound. There is redness and swelling over the lower extremity. Imaging of the limb shows signs consistent with vascular issues. The patient is admitted, and treatment begins. However, the patient's condition rapidly declines after admission.
