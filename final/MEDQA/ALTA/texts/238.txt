A young child is brought to the clinic by their parents because they have not been themselves lately and reportedly get tired very easily during physical activities compared to their peers. They also experience occasional shortness of breath. The parents also report that the child seems to be slightly shorter than other children their age. Vital signs show an average body temperature, unremarkable blood pressure, and a normal respiratory rate. On auscultation, a harsh heart murmur is heard. The murmur's characteristics change with posture. A cardiac assessment is conducted.
