An elderly person is brought to the physician by a family member because of increasing forgetfulness over the past few years. Initially, they used to forget familiar names or to lock the house door. Recently, they have encountered difficulties while returning home from the grocery store. One time, they went to a public place and returned home alone without realizing they had forgotten their companion. Their family member says that they have withdrawn from activities. They do not sleep well at night. Their family member has arranged for assistance to manage household tasks. On mental status examination, they are oriented only to basic surroundings. They describe their mood as average. Memory deficits are present. They appear indifferent about their memory lapses and say this is typical as they get older.
