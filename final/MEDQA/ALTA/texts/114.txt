A newborn is brought to the physician because of yellowing of the skin and sclerae for several hours. The infant had previously been well. The child was born at full term and had a healthy birth weight. The mother has no medical insurance and did not receive prenatal care. An older sibling has a genetic condition affecting red blood cells. Examination shows jaundice. The abdomen is mildly distended. The liver is palpated 1 cm below the right costal margin and the spleen tip is palpated just below the left costal margin. Laboratory studies show: Hemoglobin slightly below normal Reticulocytes elevated, Leukocytes within normal range, Platelets within normal range Serum Bilirubin, total elevated Direct bilirubin slightly elevated.
