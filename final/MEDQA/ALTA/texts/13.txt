An infant is brought to a medical facility because of respiratory symptoms. The infant has had cold symptoms and elevated temperature for a short time. Recently, the infant began making unusual breathing sounds and seemed to be having some trouble breathing. The infant is alert and does not appear to be in severe distress. Vital signs indicate a fever, increased respiratory rate, and good oxygen levels. There is a noticeable difference in breathing sounds during the examination. The throat shows mild changes but no alarming signs. Imaging of the chest is obtained.
