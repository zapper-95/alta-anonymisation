A male with a past medical history of heart issues presents to the emergency room with shortness of breath. He notes that he stopped taking his diuretic medication about two weeks ago, because he ran out of pills. On exam, his oxygen saturation is low, his lungs have crackles, and he has elevated jugular venous pulsation. Despite these symptoms, normal EKG and troponin levels rule out an acute cardiac event. The presence of crackles in the lungs and jugular venous distension suggests significant vascular congestion.
