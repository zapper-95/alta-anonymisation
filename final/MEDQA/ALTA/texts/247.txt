A middle-aged person presents to the emergency department due to severe pain in a joint. This pain started acutely, and they describe the pain as sharp. Their medical history is significant for several chronic conditions. They take a number of medications but do not remember what they are. On physical exam, the affected joint appears warm, swollen, and painful to the touch. After resolution of this acute episode, the patient is started on a medication to manage future symptoms. One month later on follow-up, they are found to have a blood cell irregularity.
