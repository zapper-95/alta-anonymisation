A person presents with urinary incontinence and difficulty urinating. They report not urinating for the past 2 days and experiencing urine leakage throughout the day. The medical history includes cardiovascular conditions, metabolic issues, and psychological health concerns. Medications are prescribed for managing chronic conditions, including heart and metabolic health. Vital signs show stable measurements within normal ranges. The physical examination reveals lower abdominal discomfort. The neurological examination shows decreased reflexes. On gynecologic examination, no issues were detected. An ultrasound examination showed a large urinary bladder with no structural abnormalities.
