A few days after undergoing a surgical procedure while abroad, an individual develops symptoms that include fever, vomiting, and jaundice. During the procedure, they required a blood transfusion. Upon stabilization, they were transported back home. The individual exhibits jaundice and experiences tenderness over the liver area during an examination. Laboratory tests reveal indications of significant liver issues, including increased bilirubin levels and signs of a serious liver condition possibly related to hepatitis exposure. An ultrasound confirms liver enlargement, and a biopsy indicates extensive liver damage.
