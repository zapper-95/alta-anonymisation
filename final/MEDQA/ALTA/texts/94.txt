A person, who recently migrated, presents to a physician with respiratory symptoms and weight loss. Tests confirm the diagnosis of tuberculosis. The individual is started on a regimen of tuberculosis drugs and later on antiretroviral therapy. They report improvement. After a few weeks, the treatment regimen is adjusted, and additional therapy is added. The person presents again with a fever and increased cough. There is no respiratory distress but generalized lymphadenopathy is present. Changes are observed in the subsequent chest radiograph, and follow-up tests show improvements in the condition.
