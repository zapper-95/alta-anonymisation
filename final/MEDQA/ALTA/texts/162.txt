A patient presents to their physician’s office after noticing a lump some time ago. The lump seemed to change and became tender at times. It is otherwise painless, and the patient denies any discharge or skin changes. They have a family member with a history of a serious illness. The patient is active and denies tobacco, alcohol, or illicit drug use. On exam, the lump is palpated during the exam. There is slight tenderness to deep palpation of the lump. There is no significant swelling in the lymph nodes.
