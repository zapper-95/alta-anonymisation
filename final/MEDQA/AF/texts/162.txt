An individual presents to their healthcare provider's office after noticing a round lump in the torso area a few months ago. They report that the lump seemed to enlarge and became tender just preceding recent periods of stress. It is otherwise painless, and the individual denies any discharge or skin changes. They have no significant past medical history. The individual does not report any substance use. On exam, the torso appears symmetric and normal. A 3-cm round, mobile mass is palpated in the upper outer quadrant of the torso. There is slight tenderness to deep palpation of the mass. There is no lymphadenopathy on either side.
