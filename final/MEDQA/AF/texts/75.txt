An individual is brought to a medical facility after an incident. The patient’s vitals are as follows: blood pressure is low, heart rate is elevated, respiratory rate is high, and temperature is normal. On physical examination, the patient is unconscious with a reduced GCS score and is cyanotic. There are multiple injuries, including fractures, dislocations, contusions, and a puncture wound. There are no breath sounds on one side and there is hyperresonance to percussion on the same side. Preparations are made for an emergency procedure to be performed to treat this patient’s likely condition.
