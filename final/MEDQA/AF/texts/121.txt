An individual presents to their healthcare provider with complaints of general discomfort, tiredness, sore throat, and fever over the last several days. Their vital signs are as follows: T 39.1 C, HR 82, BP 122/76, RR 14, and SpO2 99%. Physical examination is significant for an enlarged spleen, throat inflammation, and swollen lymph nodes. The tonsils are not notably enlarged. Laboratory work-up shows an elevated white blood cell count, atypical cells on a peripheral blood smear, and a positive antibody screen.
