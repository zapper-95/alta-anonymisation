An individual presents to their primary care physician for evaluation of a health concern. They have not experienced any significant changes in their health status recently. They first started experiencing this health issue some time ago and it has been unremarkable up until it changed recently. Their past medical history includes fractures sustained in an accident some time ago. They didn't see the accident coming and have had increasing difficulty noticing objects in their peripheral vision. They also note having recurrent headaches in the past. Otherwise, they have been healthy with no previous significant medical events. Physical exam reveals unexpected symptoms and visual field defects.
