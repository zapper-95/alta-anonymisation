An individual presents to their healthcare provider for urinary issues. They have been waking up every morning with their clothing soaked. They notice that if they do not take regular bathroom breaks, their clothing will have some moisture in it. They urinate multiple times per day but claim it is a small volume. Current medications include several for ongoing health issues. Their temperature is 36.8°C, blood pressure is elevated, pulse is 90/min, respirations are 15/min, and oxygen saturation is 99% on room air. Physical exam is notable for decreased sensation in the lower extremities and a heart condition.
