A patient sees a healthcare provider for an evaluation. Some time ago, they presented with complaints about facial issues. It was noticed that one side of the patient's face has some muscle weakness. Since then, the patient feels like their facial muscles have gotten weaker. They were given a course of treatment, but have still not recovered fully. Upon being asked to close their eyes, the patient has difficulty doing so. On examination, there is no loss of pain, temperature, or crude or tactile touch sensations in the whole face. The exam is otherwise unremarkable. The healthcare provider recommends a therapy that will help relieve their symptoms.
