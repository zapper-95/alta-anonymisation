An individual visits a healthcare facility to follow up on ongoing stomach discomfort. Previously, it was noted that there had been discomfort in the upper abdomen. There had never been any vomiting of blood and no substantial weight loss. No medications were taken, and there was no specific family medical history mentioned. At that time, a healthcare provider suggested starting on a general treatment for stomach issues. Today, despite this treatment, there is still discomfort. Hearing this, the healthcare provider decides to order a non-invasive test.
