An individual presents to the emergency department with shortness of breath after exposure to cold air. The person informs the physician about having a respiratory condition but mentions not using prescribed treatments regularly. The individual does not smoke and occasionally consumes alcohol. On physical examination, the temperature is 37.0°C, the pulse is 120/min, the blood pressure is 114/76 mm Hg, and the respiratory rate is 32/min. Auscultation of the chest reveals bilateral wheezing. The physician asks the nurse to administer a bronchodilator; however, the individual declines due to previous experiences. The physician then prescribes an alternative medication, which results in clinical improvement.
