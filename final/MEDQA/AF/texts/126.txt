A patient comes to the physician because of intermittent episodes of heart palpitations for several weeks. The palpitations are accompanied by chest discomfort. The patient does not smoke. They appear nervous. Their pulse is 110/min and irregular, and blood pressure is within normal range. Examination shows a slight tremor on both hands. There is a murmur heard along the sternal border. The extremities are warm, and pulses are 2+ bilaterally.
