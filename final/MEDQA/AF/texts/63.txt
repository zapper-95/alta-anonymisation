An individual is brought to the physician by a companion because of increasing forgetfulness for some time. Initially, they used to forget people's names or forget to lock the house door. During the past year, they have gotten lost twice while returning from errands and were unable to tell passersby their address. One time, they went out with someone and returned home alone without realizing they had forgotten them. The companion says that over the past year, they have withdrawn from social gatherings and avoid making conversation. They do not sleep well at night. The individual receives assistance with some tasks at home. On mental status examination, they are oriented only to person. They describe their mood as fair. Short- and long-term memory deficits are present. They appear indifferent about their memory lapses and say this is normal for some people.
