An individual comes to the physician because they are experiencing difficulties during sexual intercourse. They first noticed the problem several months ago. They state that their personal life is satisfactory, but their inability to engage in sexual intercourse has been frustrating. They have a chronic health condition and were diagnosed with a mental health disorder some years ago. They have had a great deal of stress lately. They had not been active for some time before their current situation. They do not smoke. They consume alcohol occasionally. Current medications include treatments for their chronic condition and mental health disorder. Their temperature is 37.5°C (99.5°F), pulse is 80/min, and blood pressure is 135/82 mm Hg. BMI is 30 kg/m2. Pedal pulses are difficult to palpate. The remainder of the physical examination shows no abnormalities.
