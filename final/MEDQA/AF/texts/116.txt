A patient presents with a generalized, non-itchy rash for a short duration. The patient has also had a fever and sore throat for several days. Examination shows facial redness except around the mouth. A diffuse, papular, erythematous rash that blanches with pressure is seen over the body. Pharyngeal erythema and a red tongue are noted. The leukocyte count is elevated with a predominance of segmented neutrophils and lymphocytes.
