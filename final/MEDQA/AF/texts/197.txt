A young child is brought to the physician by an individual because of a recent history of recurrent nonbilious vomiting. Vomiting occurs almost immediately after most feeds. Each time, the child becomes irritable while feeding and refuses to finish the bottle. The child was born at term and currently weighs a typical amount for their age. Vaccinations are up-to-date. The child appears agitated and cries during the examination. The temperature, pulse, respirations, and blood pressure are within normal ranges. Examination shows no specific abnormalities. The abdomen is soft and nontender. Cardiac examination shows no abnormalities. Arterial blood gas analysis is within normal limits.
