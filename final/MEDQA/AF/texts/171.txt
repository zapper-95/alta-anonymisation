A patient comes to the physician for a check-up. On questioning, the patient has had some difficulty speaking for several months. During a conversation, there is often difficulty repeating what the interlocutor has said. Vital signs are within normal limits. The patient speaks in sentences and demonstrates understanding. When asked to say a specific word, the patient replies with, “Flargen… no, that's wrong…blargen, no that isn't it either...largen...no? How about, [correct word]?”
