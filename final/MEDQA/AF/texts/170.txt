An individual presents with symptoms including discharge, itching, and painful urination for the past 5 days. The patient reports occasional use of protective measures. The patient denies any fever, chills, abdominal pain, excessive bleeding, or flank pain. The individual is afebrile and the vital signs are within normal limits. Examination reveals erythema with a profuse, purulent discharge. The pH level is 7.0. Swab and urine samples are obtained for microscopy and culture, and results are pending.
