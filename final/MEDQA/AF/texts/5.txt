An individual presents to their healthcare provider with a neck mass. They first noticed a firm mass on the anterior aspect of their neck approximately several months ago. The mass is painless and has not increased in size since then. They have also noticed occasional fatigue and have gained weight in the past few months despite no change in diet or exercise frequency. Their past medical history is notable for a condition. They deny any prior thyroid disorder. They do not smoke and rarely consume alcohol. Their temperature is within normal range, blood pressure is 127/72 mmHg, pulse is 87/min, and respirations are 19/min. On exam, they have a firm, symmetric, and stone-firm thyroid.
