An individual is observing the development of a new biological occurrence. After a detailed analysis of the occurrence and its characteristics, they suggest an idea: Initially, a host cell is affected by multiple agents from the same family. Within the host cell, concomitant production of various components from both agents occurs. Ultimately, the different components from the agents are combined into a unique and novel entity. The newly formed entity is both stable and viable and is a new variant from the family that caused the occurrence.
