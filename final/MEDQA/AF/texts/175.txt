An individual is brought to the emergency department after the sudden onset of abdominal pain and vomiting. The patient also manifests symptoms of redness and swelling of the face and lips without pruritus. The symptoms began following a medical procedure. Vital signs are within normal limits. Examination shows facial edema, erythema of the oral mucosa, and an enlarged tongue. The abdomen is soft, and there is tenderness to palpation over the lower quadrants. An abdominal ultrasound shows segmental thickening of the intestinal wall.
