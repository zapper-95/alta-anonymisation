An individual is brought to a healthcare provider for evaluation because there have been concerns about a number of troubling signs. Specifically, it has been noticed that the individual has been exhibiting aggressive behavior as well as hyperactivity. In addition, the individual has had challenges with certain physical characteristics. Physical exam reveals distinctive facial features, reduced height, and joint stiffness. Eye exam reveals no abnormalities of the cornea or lens. Based on these findings, the patient is referred to a geneticist for further evaluation.
