An individual presents to a physician because of a history of cough, fever, and weight loss. Their tests confirm the diagnosis of a respiratory infection. They are started on a treatment regimen consisting of appropriate medications. They report significant improvement in symptoms after a few weeks. After another few weeks, the physician adjusts the regimen and adds additional therapy. After a few weeks, the patient presents with complaints of fever and increased cough for a few days. There is no respiratory distress but generalized lymphadenopathy is present. Further tests show pulmonary infiltrates and mediastinal lymphadenopathy.
