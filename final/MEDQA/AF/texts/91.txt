An individual presents with abdominal pain. They say that the pain “comes and goes” throughout the day and usually lasts 20-30 minutes per episode. They can point to the spot 1-2 inches above the umbilicus where they feel the pain. They deny any feeling of regurgitation or nighttime cough but endorse nausea. They report that certain changes have improved the pain. They have not noticed any diarrhea or change in stools. On physical exam, they are tender to palpation above the umbilicus. Bowel sounds are present. A stool guaiac test is positive. Further diagnostic procedures are planned to diagnose their condition.
