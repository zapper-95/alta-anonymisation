A patient presents to a physician for worsening shortness of breath and increasing abdominal distention over the last few months. The shortness of breath is worse on exertion and improves with rest. The patient now experiences difficulty during physical activity. The patient's medical history is otherwise unremarkable. The physical examination reveals abdominal distention and visibly engorged periumbilical veins. Bilateral pitting edema is noted around the ankles. The finger-prick blood glucose level is within normal range.
