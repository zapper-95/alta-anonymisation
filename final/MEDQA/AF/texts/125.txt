An individual is visiting the doctor for a routine check-up. The individual's height and weight are appropriate, and they are up to date on all vaccinations with no medical complaints. The individual is doing well and was offered a different opportunity but declined. The individual is reserved with the physician. Afterwards, a relative asks to speak to the physician alone and expresses concern that the individual prefers to spend time alone and feels disconnected. The relative is also worried that the individual is sensitive to feedback. The individual has interests but hasn’t participated in any activities because they feel hesitant and think nobody wants to hang out with them. When encouraged to invite friends over, the individual becomes upset and says, “Nobody wants to be friends with me, I am too uninteresting and I just know I’ll end up getting rejected.”
