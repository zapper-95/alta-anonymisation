An individual presents to their physician for a medical appointment. They complain of a rash involving their chest, face, and arms, a watery nasal discharge, and mild bilateral knee pain. They have had these symptoms for about 5 days. The symptoms do not seem to bother them too much, but they are concerned about their health. They mention having been in contact with someone who was unwell about 10 days ago. They are unsure about their health history. Their vital signs are within normal limits. Examination shows a moderately dense maculopapular lacy rash spread over the patient’s trunk, extremities, and face. No lymph node, liver, or spleen enlargement is noted. The knee joints appear normal.
