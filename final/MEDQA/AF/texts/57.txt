A child is brought to the doctor by caregivers. They are concerned that the child becomes lethargic and irritated between meals. They found that feeding them often with small servings helps. The child was born full term and is meeting all developmental milestones. Today, the child's blood pressure is 55/33 mm Hg, heart rate is 120/min, respiratory rate is 40/min, and temperature is 37.0°C (98.6°F). On physical exam, the child is irritated. They are slightly jaundiced. The heart has a regular rate and rhythm and the lungs are clear to auscultation bilaterally. Blood work shows normocytic anemia with elevated reticulocyte count and decreased haptoglobin. Sickle cell anemia and other hemoglobinopathies are also ruled out. A Coombs test is negative. Red blood cell osmotic fragility gap is normal. The physician determined that these findings are related to an enzyme deficiency.
