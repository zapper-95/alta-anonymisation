A patient is brought to a medical facility by emergency services after an accident. They are unconscious upon arrival and are found to have severe internal bleeding due to trauma. They undergo urgent medical intervention and severely damaged tissues are removed. After the procedure, the patient is taken into a specialized care unit for recovery. Over the subsequent week, serial labs are drawn showing target cells, thrombocytosis, and leukocytosis. They are started on a prophylactic regimen that is continued upon release.
