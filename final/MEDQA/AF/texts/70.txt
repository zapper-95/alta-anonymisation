An individual presents to a healthcare facility with the complaints of fever, malaise, and night sweats. The patient recently observed an enlargement of lymph nodes, which are examined on a regular basis. The patient has a medical history that includes previous treatment for a health condition. On physical exam, several lymph nodes are palpable with a large non-tender palpable mass. Fine-needle aspiration demonstrates what the pathologist describes as "a cell presence, suspicious for a type of hematologic disorder."
