An individual presents with sudden-onset breathing difficulties. The past medical history includes unspecified conditions. Family history is significant for a relative who died of a health issue. The patient is pale and diaphoretic. Cardiac exam shows abnormal findings. There is erythema and swelling over multiple veins in the left leg. Ultrasound of the left leg is positive for a vascular issue. The patient is admitted, and treatment is started. However, despite these interventions, the patient dies shortly after admission.
