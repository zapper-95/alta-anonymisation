An individual is brought to a healthcare facility because of a 3-week history of foot pain. Initially, the pain only occurred during activity, but now it is also present at rest. The pain is located diffusely over the forefoot. Vital signs are within normal range. Examination shows mild swelling over the distal forefoot. Pressing the metatarsal of the third toe results in pain. They walk with an antalgic gait. The remainder of the examination shows no abnormalities. An x-ray of the foot shows a slight loss of cortical density and callus formation at the third metatarsal shaft.
