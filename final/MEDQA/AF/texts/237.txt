A person presents to the emergency department with breathing difficulties. They report feeling unable to take a deep breath. There is a past medical history of unspecified health issues. Temperature is within normal range, blood pressure is elevated, pulse is rapid, respirations are increased, and oxygen saturation is low on room air. Standard emergency treatment for breathing difficulties is initiated. The individual claims they are still short of breath. Physical exam reveals breathing sounds and poor air movement. Oxygen saturation remains low.
