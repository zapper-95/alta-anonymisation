An individual is brought to the emergency department due to a change in behavior. It is reported that over the past several months, the individual has become increasingly withdrawn; they have not been in contact with others. The individual had always been reserved, needing some time alone, but used to participate in social interactions regularly. The patient says that they cannot leave the house because they believe they are being watched. The patient does not consume alcohol. Vital signs are within normal limits. Physical examination shows no abnormalities. On mental status examination, it is impossible to follow the patient's sequence of thoughts. They are anxious and have a flat affect.
