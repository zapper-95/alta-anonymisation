An individual presents to a health clinic complaining of excessive sleepiness. They first noticed the daytime sleepiness about a year ago but report that it significantly worsened over time. They mention having a varied sleep schedule and taking breaks during the day. Despite this, they wake up feeling exhausted. They easily fall asleep during activities and while focusing on tasks. This has started to affect their daily activities. On days when they have no obligations, they can sleep for extended periods. They report 2 recent episodes of sudden generalized body weakness along with ptosis and difficulty speaking that occurred after becoming angry. Each episode lasted about 10 seconds, but they felt back to normal within 15 minutes without any persistent deficits. Their Epworth Sleepiness Scale score is 17.
