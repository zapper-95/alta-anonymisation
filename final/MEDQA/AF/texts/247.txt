An individual presents to the emergency department due to severe pain in a joint. This pain started acutely, and they describe the pain as sharp. The medical history includes various health conditions. They take medications but do not remember what they are. On physical exam, the affected joint appears warm, swollen, erythematous, and exquisitely painful to the touch. After resolution of this acute episode, the patient is started on a drug to prevent recurrence of the symptoms. One month later on follow-up, they are found to have a blood disorder.
