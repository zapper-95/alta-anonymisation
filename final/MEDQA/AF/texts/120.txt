A patient comes to the physician because of worsening involuntary movements of their arms for the past several months. They report that certain tasks have become more difficult to perform. The patient appears anxious. Vital signs are within normal limits. Examination shows a low-amplitude tremor when the arms are outstretched that worsens when reaching out to grab an object. Muscle strength and tone is normal. Deep tendon reflexes are 2+. On mental status examination, their mood is good. Their speech is normal in rate and rhythm.
