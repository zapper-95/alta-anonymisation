An individual presents with urinary issues, including leakage and discomfort in the lower abdominal region. They report that they have not urinated for the past 2 days and that the urine leakage occurs during both day and night and is not associated with physical exertion. Their vital signs are within normal ranges. The physical examination reveals lower abdominal tenderness with a distended urinary bladder that is palpated in the lower abdominal region. The neurological examination shows decreased reflexes bilaterally, and diminished fine touch and vibratory sensation. An ultrasound examination showed an overdistended urinary bladder with no structural abnormalities.
