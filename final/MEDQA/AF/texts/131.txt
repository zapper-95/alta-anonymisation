A patient presents to the healthcare provider's office for severe abdominal cramping. The patient has experienced irregular health patterns for two years, which then became roughly regular every few weeks, but the patient has had extremely painful cramps and often has to miss activities. The cramps are intermittent and feel like a dull ache in the center of the abdomen, and there is usually accompanying back pain. The patient has been using heating pads with limited relief. Some health episodes are reported as “really intense” and require additional care every few hours for a couple of days each occurrence, but this does not limit activities. There are no bowel or bladder changes reported. The patient had normal development throughout childhood. On exam, the patient is of average height and weight. The patient is well appearing and has no abdominal tenderness. Normal development is noted.
