An individual is brought to the emergency department because of worsening shortness of breath for several hours. For the past few days, they have had intermittent chest pain that worsens with lying down or taking deep breaths. They have also had a runny nose and a dry cough for a couple of weeks. Their current medications include treatments for unspecified conditions. They appear unwell. Their vital signs show elevated heart rate and breathing rate, with variations in blood pressure during breathing. Examination shows signs of fluid retention.
