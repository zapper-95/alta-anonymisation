An individual is brought in by emergency services. The patient was involved in an accident and sustained injuries. Upon arrival to the trauma bay, the patient is assessed and requires immediate intubation. The patient is noted to have multiple bruises over their body, as well as small red or purple spots and larger purple areas. There is a wound over the shoulder area that continues to bleed despite a pressure bandage. Imaging of the lower extremity shows multiple fractures. Two large bore IV lines are placed, and the patient shows signs of bleeding from around the sites of venepuncture. Labs are notable for elevated white blood cell count, low hemoglobin, low platelets, and prolonged clotting times.
