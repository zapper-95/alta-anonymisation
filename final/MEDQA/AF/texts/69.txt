An individual presents to their healthcare provider complaining of several days of fever, malaise, dyspnea, and productive cough and has recently developed pleuritic chest pain. The individual's past medical history is notable for a respiratory condition and uses medication as needed. The individual does not smoke. The temperature is 101°F (38.3°C), blood pressure is 115/75 mmHg, pulse is 110/min, and respirations are 21/min. Rales are noted in the right lower lung, and a chest radiograph demonstrates focal consolidation in the right lower lung lobe. After initiating the appropriate treatment, the individual agrees to further medical evaluation. A protein is identified in the serum that is thought to be elevated in response to the condition. An amino acid sequence at the carboxy terminus of the protein is isolated and a fluorescent marker is added to the sequence. This labeled sequence is subsequently added to a gel containing a protein isolated from cell surfaces and subsequently labeled with another fluorescent marker. The sequence binds strongly to this protein as evidenced by increased fluorescence in the gel.
