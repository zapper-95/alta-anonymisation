A patient comes to the physician for a follow-up examination. A previous test showed atypical results of undetermined significance, but further testing was negative at that time. On questioning, the patient has had fatigue and an increase in abdominal girth despite a weight loss over the past several months. The patient has a history of chronic medical conditions. The patient has been experiencing these symptoms for several years. Current medications include treatment for these conditions. Abdominal examination shows shifting dullness. There is tenderness to palpation of the right lower quadrant but no guarding or rebound. Palpation shows a small mass in the lower abdomen.
