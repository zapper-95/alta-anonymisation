An individual is brought to the physician because of fever, chills, abdominal pain, and profuse non-bloody diarrhea. The symptoms began one week ago, when there were several days of low-grade fever and constipation. The individual recently returned from a trip, where time was spent with others. The temperature is 39.3°C (102.8°F). Examination shows diffuse abdominal tenderness and mild hepatosplenomegaly. There is a faint salmon-colored maculopapular rash on the trunk and abdomen.
