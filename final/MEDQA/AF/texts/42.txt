An individual comes to the physician with right-sided, dull, throbbing abdominal pain for 12 hours. The patient has also had a burning sensation on urination and increased urinary frequency for the past 3 days. There has been no unusual discharge. Vital signs are within normal limits. Physical examination shows tenderness in the lower abdomen; lifting the area provides relief. The genital area appears normal, with no discharge at the meatus. Laboratory studies show a normal complete blood count; urinalysis shows 3 WBC/hpf. A Gram stain of a swab shows polymorphonuclear leukocytes but no organisms. An ultrasound shows increased blood flow to the affected area compared to the other side.
