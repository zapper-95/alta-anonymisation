A person presents to a mental health professional requesting help with compulsions as they are interfering with daily life. The person explains a fear that something undesirable will happen if they do not check various household items before leaving. They state that everything must be checked multiple times in a specific order, and if the order is disrupted, they must start over. They have recently faced issues due to being late and have experienced personal difficulties as a result of these habits. The professional explained that cognitive-behavioral therapy may be of assistance and also suggested medication.
