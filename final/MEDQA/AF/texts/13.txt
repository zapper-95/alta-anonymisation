A person is brought for evaluation because of a cough that the individual(s) are worried about. The person has had a runny nose and a low-grade fever for the past 2 days, with some hoarseness and a rough-sounding cough that started this afternoon. This evening, the person began making some high-pitched sounds when taking breaths, and seemed to be having some trouble breathing. The person is alert and does not appear to be in acute distress. The person has a temperature of 38.0°C (100.4 °F), with a respiratory rate of 50/min and O2 saturation of 97%. There is audible inspiratory stridor that worsens when the person starts to cry during the examination. The person has an occasional barking cough. The pharynx is mildly erythematous with normal tonsils and no exudate. A frontal X-ray of the upper chest airways is obtained (shown in the image).
