An individual presents to a medical professional with a complaint of discomfort that has been present for some time but has worsened recently. They report discomfort with certain activities and with extended movement. The discomfort worsens with activity throughout the day and is alleviated by periods of rest. They state that there is minimal stiffness, lasting a short time after waking up most days. Physical examination reveals tenderness to palpation of the structures as well as a decreased range of motion, limited at the extremes of movement. Both areas are cool to touch and exhibit enlargement upon palpation.
