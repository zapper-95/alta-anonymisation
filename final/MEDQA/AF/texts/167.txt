An individual presented to the emergency room due to progressive breathing difficulties. The individual has a history of a chronic health condition. The individual reports experiencing difficulty breathing when lying down, sudden nighttime breathing difficulties, and general tiredness. On physical examination, the blood pressure is 80/50 mm Hg. There is prominent neck vein distention. An abnormal heart sound, lung sounds, and swelling in both legs were also detected. A 2d echo was performed, which showed a reduced heart function.
