An individual presents to establish care with a family physician after experiencing progressively worsening back pain. They have not had any checkups in recent times. They first started having back pain some time ago, but the pain has begun to be severe in the mornings. They are no longer able to get relief with over-the-counter medications. They also feel stiff every morning, and this usually lasts between 30 minutes and an hour. Both knees are also very painful, particularly upon standing up from a seated position. The pain improves when they move around, so they try to be somewhat physically active. They also report that they cannot use their hands for long periods of time due to joint pain and stiffness. A history of similar health issues is noted. Upon physical examination, some joints are warm and swollen.
