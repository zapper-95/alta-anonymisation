An older adult man presents in a healthcare setting with a condition affecting his sexual health. Recently, he has been unable to engage in intimate activities. Despite this, he shows a moderate level of interest in sexual matters. Additionally, he experiences a prickly sensation in the buttocks and cramping pain in the thighs when walking, which lessens during periods of inactivity. He does not experience a startle response that wakes him up at night, nor does he have an automatic startle response in the morning. He has a history of hormonal imbalances, a ventricular septal defect, and hypertension. He also has a history of tobacco use. Upon examination, he is found to have diminished distal foot and brachial pulses.
