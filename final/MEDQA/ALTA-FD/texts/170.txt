A 21-year-old woman presents with symptoms that have persisted over the past week. She reports experiencing a burning sensation during urination and tingling. Additionally, she notes vaginal discharge that is notable for its milky, viscous consistency and fishy smell. The discharge has caused her irritation. Despite these concerns, the woman denies having a fever or abdominal pain, and her vital signs are within normal limits. Her last menstrual period occurred in recent weeks. The woman is sexually active with occasional partners and uses barrier protection irregularly. A pelvic examination was conducted to obtain samples, as well as bladder samples, for laboratory analysis and pathogen testing. Her vaginal pH was found to be slightly acidic. The results of the laboratory tests are still pending.
