A young girl was brought to the hospital with a hoarse voice and has had a runny nose for the past two days. She has experienced a moderate fever for the past four days and exhibits vocal tension. The girl developed a throaty cough starting last evening and began making croaking sounds when taking breaths this afternoon. Despite these symptoms, she is alert and does not appear to be in acute distress. She had seemed to be breathing with difficulty. Her temperature is recorded at 40.0°C, her respiratory rate is 48/min, and her oxygen saturation is at 90%. She has altered respiratory noises which worsen when she begins to laugh. Additionally, she has a brassy cough. Examination reveals that her pharynx is slightly enlarged and her tonsils are slightly swollen, but there is no exudate present. A general chest imaging was conducted to further assess her condition.
