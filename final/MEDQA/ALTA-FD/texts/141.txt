A 44-year-old man visits a medical facility with altered mental status. This individual has a history of vascular disease related to substance use and has been facing more frequent bouts of forgetfulness alongside increasing levels of disorientation. His respirations are 19 per minute, and his blood pressure is slightly elevated. Despite these issues, his body temperature remains normal. His pulse is recorded at 88 per minute, and his oxygen saturation is at a normal level on room air. The man experiences some tenderness in the abdomen and exhibits a type of unsteady gait. Lab results indicate a serum potassium level of 3.4 mEq/L and previously recorded at 3.7 mEq/L, which may contribute to his condition. The sodium and calcium levels are within the normal range, while chloride levels are slightly below typical. Bicarbonate levels are typical, although his BUN level is above normal. The serum glucose is measured at 65 mg/dL, and the creatinine levels are standard for kidney function.
