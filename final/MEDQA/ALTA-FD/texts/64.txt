A 65-year-old individual presented to the physician for further medical assessment due to existing nerve damage. The serum protein electrophoresis results showed increased albumin levels, while tests indicated a mildly reduced white blood cell count. However, serum creatinine and serum electrolyte concentrations remain within the reference range. A bone marrow biopsy revealed abnormalities, including downregulation of Class II major histocompatibility molecules in atypical plasma cells.
