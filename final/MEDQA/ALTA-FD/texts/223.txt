A middle-aged woman presents with shortness of breath, which is exacerbated when climbing stairs. Approximately eight days ago, she experienced unexplained blurred vision. Her medical history includes mild seasonal allergies, and her family history does not include heart disease. During examination, she has a systolic murmur that is noticeable when sitting up and mild swelling in the legs. There is wheezing observed in the chest examination that does not clear with deep breaths. A chest X-ray reveals a non-specific lung condition. Additionally, a 2-dimensional echocardiogram shows an irregularity in cardiac muscle movement near the coronary sinus.
