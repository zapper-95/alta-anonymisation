A 20-year-old man was evaluated in a healthcare facility after experiencing equilibrium disturbances, with sudden episodes of dizziness and digestive discomfort. For the past week, he had frequent episodes of coughing, muscle weakness, and a patchy rash, leading to complaints of feeling sluggish. Despite no family history of serious illness, he appeared visibly fatigued with an intermittent cough. His temperature was noted to be 38.4°C, with a pulse of 76/min and blood pressure recorded at 87/54 mm Hg. A cardiac examination revealed minor irregularities. On physical examination, he exhibited localized spots on the trunk, a blotchy rash on the limbs, and showed signs of dermal exfoliation on the knuckles and shedding of skin on the toes. Laboratory tests indicated hemoglobin levels at 15.9 g/dL, a leukocyte count of 11,200/mm3, and a platelet count of 150,000/mm3. Serum tests showed urea nitrogen at 40 mg/dL, glucose at 95 mg/dL, creatinine at 1.5 mg/dL, and total bilirubin at 1.9 mg/dL, with AST and ALT levels recorded at 105 U/L and 135 U/L, respectively. The urinalysis showed no abnormalities.
