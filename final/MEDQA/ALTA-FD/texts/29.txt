A 14-year-old female was brought to the physician by her mother. She is experiencing fever, chills, gastric discomfort, and severe watery diarrhea. These symptoms began several days ago, following an episode of moderate fever and diarrhea that occurred one week ago. The young girl returned from Thailand 3 weeks ago. Upon examination, her temperature is elevated, and she exhibits localized abdominal tenderness, significant lymphadenopathy, and a faint rose-colored maculopapular rash on her arms.
