A microbiologist is studying the adaptation of viral proteins and proposes a theory related to viral evolution. The theory involves a host cell co-infected with viruses from a similar classification. Within the host cell, there are observed variations in viral gene expression dynamics. This process leads to the integration of different genome segments into a reassembled virus form. The resulting newly formed virus particle demonstrates robustness and lingering infectivity. This particle originates from a virus lineage linked to the outbreak.
