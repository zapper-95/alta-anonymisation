A 22-year-old man was brought to the emergency department by his uncle. Over the past 7 months, he has experienced a change in personality, becoming increasingly withdrawn. He has not answered any phone calls or visited his family and friends, despite previously meeting with friends at least once a week. He expresses beliefs that the government is monitoring him through radio waves and that secret agencies are extracting his thoughts. He does not consume coffee and used to chew tobacco occasionally. He quit drinking alcohol 9 months ago. His vital signs and physical examination are unremarkable. However, his mental status examination reveals it is impossible to follow his sequence of thoughts. Additionally, he is anxious and exhibits a slow speech pattern.
