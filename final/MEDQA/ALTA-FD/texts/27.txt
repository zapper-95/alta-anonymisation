An older adult male presents with abdominal pain and constipation, reporting a 4-day history of increasing left lower quadrant pain and having had only two small bowel movements in this time. Normally, he reports having a bowel movement every other day. He has unregulated hypertension and hyperlipidemia and is taking enalapril, furosemide, ibuprofen, and atorvastatin. The individual also has a smoking habit and consumes alcohol, with a diet primarily consisting of fast food. On examination, his temperature is 100.4°F (38°C), blood pressure is 155/90 mmHg, pulse is 75/min, and respirations are 16/min. Previous colonoscopy results revealed gastrointestinal irregularities and digestive tract anomalies, but no serious abnormalities were noted.
