An 85-year-old male, who is obese, experienced an unexpected onset of symptoms including shortness of breath and a cough while resting. He visited an urgent care clinic to address these symptoms. His medical history includes type II diabetes, for which he is taking glipizide and insulin, and he is also on a beta-blocker. Additionally, he takes a vitamin D supplement. Upon examination, he was found to have bipedal edema and a delay in capillary refill. The medical team decided to start him on a high-flow nasal cannula and his symptoms stabilized quickly. Laboratory tests revealed serum sodium at 140 mEq/L, potassium at 4.1 mEq/L, chloride at 97 mEq/L, bicarbonate at 27 mEq/L, blood urea nitrogen at 24 mg/dL, serum glucose at 170 mg/dL, serum creatinine at 1.5 mg/dL, and serum calcium at 9.7 mg/dL.
