A young boy was brought to the emergency room by a family friend. The boy uses a mobility aid and was being monitored as he had been disruptive, attempting to throw objects at others. Recently, the boy experienced a tragic incident; his parents died in a house fire. Following this, he stopped taking his medications after his parents fell ill. His medications include vitamin C, ibuprofen, and lorazepam, which he had been taking for a long time. Unfortunately, the family friend was unable to provide any medical records. The boy had been acting unusually for a few days, showing behaviors such as squinting, unexpected jerks, and rapid foot tapping. He also lost several teeth in early childhood. To help him relax, an injectable drug was administered, and blood was drawn for laboratory tests.
