A 39-year-old man presents to the emergency department with altered mental status. He was found unresponsive in an industrial storage yard where he works as a railway maintenance technician. His recent medical history includes undergoing anesthesia for a hernia repair. The patient has a past medical history of borderline personality disorder and is currently taking olanzapine, carbamazepine, and atenolol. Additionally, he suffers from migraines, depression, and hypothyroidism. On examination, his vital signs show an elevated temperature, low blood pressure, a very high pulse, and an increased breathing rate. His oxygen saturation is slightly reduced, and his level of consciousness is decreased. His muscle strength is extremely weak, and he is incoherent when answering questions. He is covered in sweat and mud.
