A 17-year-old college student reports excessive sleepiness, first noticed several months ago with a worsening pattern after starting college. The student goes to bed around 1 a.m. and wakes up for class at 6 a.m., often needing to nap between classes for a total of three hours during the day. Despite this, the student wakes up feeling exhausted and easily falls asleep during class and while studying. This sleepiness has affected both their focus on academics and social life. On weekends, the student can sleep up to 30 hours a day. Additionally, the student reports frequent muscle fatigue and during episodes of weakness, they experience light sensitivity and physical numbness. These brief episodes typically occur after becoming excited, and the student returns to feeling normal after some time. On the Epworth Sleepiness Scale, the student's score is significantly higher than normal.
