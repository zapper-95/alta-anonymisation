A 49-year-old man is experiencing a range of personality changes and physical symptoms. He has become more skeptical, candid, exacting, and hasty in his interactions. Physically, he exhibits twitching movements of the limbs and shivering movements of the trunk. Despite these symptoms, he appears vigorous. He is also experiencing movement issues, including frequent blinking of the eye and clenching of the jaw. These physical signs are accompanied by impaired memory. It is noted that his father had cognitive issues as well.
