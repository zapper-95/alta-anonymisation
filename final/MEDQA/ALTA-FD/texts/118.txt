A 26-year-old pregnant individual at 15 weeks gestation seeks medical attention. She reports symptoms including chills without fever, pain in the knee and ankle joints, headaches, and stiffness in the shoulder, which have persisted for the past five days. Additionally, a rash is observed on her leg. A recent insect bite and a cycling trip in the park occurred a few days ago. Her past medical history includes a previous respiratory infection, and she takes a daily supplement with vitamin D and zinc. She has been receiving regular prenatal examinations, during which slightly low vitamin D levels were noted in her pregnancy. However, her obstetric examination is normal, and the fetal ultrasound shows typical measurements. The fetus has a normal heartbeat and no gross abnormalities. A sample is collected to test for a tick-borne illness.
