A child is brought to the emergency department after being involved in a skating accident. The boy is transported by fixed-wing aircraft and is unresponsive upon arrival, with considerable bleeding due to the accident. The child is taken for urgent medical attention. After receiving treatment, the child is moved into intensive care where he is kept in critical care for monitoring. Over several days, periodic labs are drawn for the boy. These labs reveal unusually shaped blood cells, lymphocytosis, and polycystosis. The child is started on a supportive care plan, and upon discharge, the boy's preventive treatment is continued.
