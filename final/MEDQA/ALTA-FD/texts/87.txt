A man in his late fifties is experiencing progressive joint pain. The swelling is noticeable over the distal interphalangeal joints of both hands as well as the carpometacarpal joints. Tenderness is present over the proximal interphalangeal joints and the wrist joints. Additionally, swelling is observed over the metatarsophalangeal joints of both feet, and tenderness is felt over the interphalangeal joints of both thumbs. He has been dealing with diabetes mellitus for 2 years, although it is not well controlled with medications. Further investigation reveals a transferrin saturation of 82% and a serum ferritin level of 800 ng/mL.
