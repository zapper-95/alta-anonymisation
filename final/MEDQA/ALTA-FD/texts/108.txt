A woman in her early fifties presents for medical consultation due to concerns regarding her left hand. She reported that she had been weaving 24 hours prior, which resulted in an abrasion over her proximal phalangeal joints. She noted discoloration and inflammation in her finger joints, as well as persistent pain in her thumb joints for several years. Additionally, she experienced pain at the bases of her fingers for multiple years. Her pain tends to intensify around midday but alleviates as the day progresses. To further evaluate her condition, the cells in her finger joints and small hand joints are studied and examined.
