A 52-year-old woman has been experiencing symptoms including weakness and frequent urination for the past 26 months. She has a history of lactose intolerance. Recent medical evaluations show a heart rate of 88/min, a respiratory rate of 17/min, a temperature of 38.0°C (100.4°F), and a blood pressure reading of 117/82 mm Hg. Laboratory results indicate that her calcium level is above the normal range and her parathyroid hormone (PTH) level is elevated. Additionally, a complete metabolic panel revealed a minor irregularity in liver enzymes. Imaging studies have demonstrated cortical bone thinning and osteopenia. Moreover, scintigraphy indicates a potential abnormality but does not provide specific insights.
