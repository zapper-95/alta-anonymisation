A 31-year-old woman was seen in a healthcare facility presenting with a fever and a 3-day history of shivering and fatigue, but no cough. She appeared ill, and her vital signs included a temperature of 37.8°C, pulse of 98/min, respirations of 16/min, and blood pressure of 110/70 mm Hg. She has a history of smoking and drinks alcohol from time to time. Her medical history includes treatment for a gland infection months ago and no current medications. Notably, she has a history of using intravenous analgesics and injecting sedatives, and physical examination showed marks consistent with injections. The lungs were clear to auscultation, but she has a heart condition with issues noted in her heart imaging, revealing slight irregularities and issues with cardiac structures. She also has some issues with the retina. Laboratory data revealed her hemoglobin level was 15.1 g/dL, leukocyte count was 10,200/mm3, platelet count was 278,000/mm3, serum glucose was 96 mg/dL, serum creatinine was 1.6 mg/dL, total bilirubin was 1.0 mg/dL, AST was 25 U/L, and ALT was 38 U/L. Two sets of blood cultures were collected for further investigation.
