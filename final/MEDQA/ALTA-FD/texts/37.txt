A 30-year-old woman, who is gravida 2 and para 1, is at late-term gestation. She is brought to a community medical center due to suspected labor, as her pregnancy was complicated by hyperglycemia issues and the fetus is larger than usual. During delivery, there is a complication in the progress of labor, necessitating the use of vacuum-assisted delivery. Following the birth, a postpartum examination reveals a marked abrasion in the birth canal. The laceration extends into the perineum at the 10 o'clock position.
