A 54-year-old man was brought to an urgent care clinic due to recurrent vomiting that started 4 hours ago. He reports the vomitus includes blood traces and unusual contents. He drinks 6–8 beers daily and seldom visits a healthcare provider. Upon examination, he appeared pale with a body temperature of 37.2°C (99.0°F) and a pulse of 114/min, along with hypotension. The physical examination revealed several issues, including an enlarged abdomen. He was oriented to the date but disoriented to location and not oriented to it again.
