A 44-year-old man from India, who is visiting a foreign country, was brought to the emergency department with short-term respiratory issues. He has a recent history of discomfort in the chest area and recurring throat irritations. Additionally, he experienced a series of muscle aches and had a fever. The man works at a factory that processes metal alloys and ceramic grain. Upon examination, a chest x-ray showed enlargement in the chest area. A sputum culture revealed the growth of gram-negative rods with cream colonies, which have irregular margins and smooth surfaces on microscopy.
