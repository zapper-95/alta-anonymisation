A 36-year-old man presented to the emergency room experiencing shortness of breath that began seven hours ago while engaged in physical activity. He has had similar symptoms in the past. Upon examination, his body temperature was 37.0°C, with a pulse rate of 104/min and a respiratory rate of 29/min. His blood pressure was recorded at 114/64 mm Hg. Notably, diminished breath sounds were observed on one side. His peak expiratory flow rate was 240 L/min and his SpO2 was 93% on room air. He was given nebulization treatment and an inhaled corticosteroid. Arterial blood gas analysis revealed a pH of 7.46, PaO2 of 64 mm Hg, PaCO2 of 27 mm Hg, and HCO3- of 24 mEq/L. After receiving oxygen by mask, his PaO2 increased to 84 mm Hg.
