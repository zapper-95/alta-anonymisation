An 11-year-old Middle Eastern boy was referred to the hospital by a medical professional. He exhibits yellowing of the eyes and has normocytic anemia. In addition to severe bone pain, he has a history of bone pain and was treated with herbal remedies. His skin appears pale and he experiences nonspecific pain in the shoulders. Inflammation is noted in his extremities, and his hands are both tender and warm. Despite these symptoms, he does not experience headaches, report joint pain, have fever, or hematuria. Laboratory results reveal a total bilirubin of 13.0 mg/dL, a white blood cell count of 14,000/mm3, a hemoglobin level of 10.4 g/dL, mean corpuscular volume of 64.7 fL, and a platelet count of 680,000/mm3. The reticulocyte count is recorded at 4.2%. A peripheral blood smear shows clumps of cells as well as erythrocytes with remnants.
