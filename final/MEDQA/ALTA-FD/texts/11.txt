A 21-year-old male was brought in by helicopter following an external incident where he was tossed by the force of the impact. He presents with a Glasgow coma scale of 8 and is currently intubated. His vitals indicate a temperature of 101.3°F and a pulse of 125 bpm, with blood pressure on the lower end of the normal range. Upon examination, he exhibits multiple ecchymoses over the body, alongside telangiectasia and ecchymosis. There is a laceration to the torso that continues to bleed despite initial treatment. Radiological assessments reveal multiple fractures in the pelvis. Intravenous access has been established, yet he shows signs of bleeding from procedure sites. Laboratory tests indicate a WBC of 17,500/mm^3, hemoglobin levels below normal limits, and platelets of 85,000/mm^3. Further analysis reveals a clotting abnormality, including a slightly extended aPTT.
