A person in their late thirties underwent surgery due to a femur fracture, which was sustained in an automobile accident. Two days after the surgery, the patient developed a fever, with a recorded temperature of 37.0°C (98.6°F). The following day, swelling was observed around the surgical site along with serous discharge and flushing of the surrounding skin. A wound culture revealed the presence of gram-negative cocci in pairs. The individual was treated with oral clindamycin; however, four days after the initiation of treatment, there was no improvement. The patient continued to have recurring chills, experience discomfort, and discharge from the wound.
