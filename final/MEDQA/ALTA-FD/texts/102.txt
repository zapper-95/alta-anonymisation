A medical researcher is studying the physiology of the immune system to better understand the effects of influenza on patients. In their research, they isolate a clump of cells, which are shown by flow cytometry to be positive for the cell surface marker CD4. The researcher then mixes this cell population with a batch of exposed cells and monitors external protein interactions. As part of their investigation, they conduct immunoprecipitation of CD4 membrane proteins and identify a glycoprotein bound to a serine/threonine kinase. Additionally, they discover that the glycoprotein bound to CD8 is composed of a single chain, which is derived from a distant cell.
