A junior orthopaedic surgery resident was completing an elbow arthroplasty procedure under the supervision of an attending physician who holds senior responsibilities in the department. During the procedure, the resident damaged a ligament, but the tendon was repaired without complication. After the surgery, the consultant assured the trainee that the recovery process for the patient would be smooth. The consultant also advised the resident to weigh the significance of this event before documenting it officially. To prevent any unease for the patient, the attending instructed the trainee to substitute the occurrence with a more pertinent event in the report.
