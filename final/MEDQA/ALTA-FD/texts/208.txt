The individual, who is middle-aged and works in the medical support field, presented to the clinic with several complaints including nausea, loss of appetite, fatigue, and a productive cough. These symptoms have persisted for an extended period. The person also reported a moderate fever and sputum that is often yellow-green. They have experienced a weight loss of 9 kg and have been suffering from profound night sweats. A radiograph revealed notable changes in the apical part of the left lung. Laboratory tests showed a hemoglobin level of 9.5 g/dL, a white blood cell count of 18,500/mm3, a neutrophil count of 27%, a lymphocyte count of 46%, an eosinophil count of 15%, and an erythrocyte sedimentation rate of 92 mm. Based on these findings, the physician suspects that the individual is suffering from a chronic sinus infection.
