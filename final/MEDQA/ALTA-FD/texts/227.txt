A 37-year-old woman, currently in her second pregnancy and at 15 weeks' gestation, presents with several symptoms following a recent return from a camping trip to Southeast Asia a month ago. She is experiencing a fever with a temperature of 39.7°C, a pulse of 95/min, respirations at 24/min, and a blood pressure of 135/88 mm Hg. The patient reports a persistent headache, nausea, abdominal discomfort, and bleeding gums after consuming spicy foods.

On clinical examination, she exhibits pallor, mild palmar erythema, and scattered ecchymoses over the trunk and back, yet has no lymphadenopathy. Laboratory findings reveal her hemoglobin level is 8.4 g/dL, leukocyte count is 10,000/mm3, and platelet count is significantly low at 21,000/mm3. There are traces of occult blood in her stool as indicated by the pathologist's report. Her coagulation profile shows an INR of 1.0, a negative Coomb's test, and no fibrin split products.

Biochemical investigations reveal the woman's serum urea is 20 mg/dL, serum creatinine is 1.1 mg/dL, total bilirubin is elevated at 3.5 mg/dL, with a direct bilirubin of 0.8 mg/dL. Her liver function tests show an alanine aminotransferase of 25 U/L, aspartate aminotransferase of 20 U/L, and lactate dehydrogenase markedly elevated at 900 U/L.

Urinalysis findings include urine protein at 1+, occasional urine white blood cells, and a high count of red blood cells at 40-50/hpf, with no bacteria present. Blood and urine cultures return negative results.
