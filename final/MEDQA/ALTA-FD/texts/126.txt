A woman in her mid-thirties experiences occasional episodes of shortness of breath and has been having heart palpitations for about a month. She reports chest pain characterized by a dull ache localized to the anterior chest region. She does not use tobacco products and drinks one to two glasses of beer on the weekends. She appears nervous, with a pulse of 100/min that is irregular. Her blood pressure is 140/90 mmHg. In addition to a slight shakiness in both hands, there is mild swelling in the toes and slight drooping of the left upper eyelid. A heart murmur is present, heard along the chest wall, while her extremities are warm. However, she has weak pulses bilaterally.
