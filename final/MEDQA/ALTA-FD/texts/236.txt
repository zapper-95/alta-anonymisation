A middle-aged woman came to the physician's office for her annual check-up visit. She reported that her health status has been stable, despite having a past medical history of a chronic health issue and heart-related problems. As a part of her check-up, she underwent basic health assessments, including a breast MRI. During this screening, a clustered microcalcification was discovered in the lower region of the breast MRI. Additionally, a mass was found during a screening mammogram. To investigate further, a diagnostic mammography was performed, followed by a biopsy. The results of the biopsy revealed medullary carcinoma of the breast.
