A 58-year-old man was brought to the emergency department with a dry cough, breathing difficulties, and a substantial fever. His symptoms began 18 days ago and he has also experienced episodes of loose bowel movements for the last 5 days. Upon examination, he appears pale. His temperature measures 39.0°C, with a pulse of 84/min and respirations at 15/min. His blood pressure is 135/80 mm Hg. There are crackles over the lung fields and his hemoglobin is 15.4 g/dL. The leukocyte count is elevated at 17,000/mm3, while his platelet count stands at 175,000/mm3. His serum sodium is 138 mEq/L, chloride is 99 mEq/L, potassium is 5.1 mEq/L, and bicarbonate is 24 mEq/L. The blood urea nitrogen level is 20 mg/dL and creatinine is 1.0 mg/dL. A chest x-ray reveals infiltrates in the lungs.
