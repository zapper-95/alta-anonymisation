A male in his sixties with a history of heart issues presented to the emergency room with shortness of breath. He reported that he stopped taking verapamil three weeks prior. Upon examination, his oxygen saturation was measured at 68%. Auscultation of his lungs revealed stridor throughout. Additionally, his jugular venous pulsation was noted to be located at the mid-epigastric region. The EKG showed normal results, and troponin levels were also found to be normal.
