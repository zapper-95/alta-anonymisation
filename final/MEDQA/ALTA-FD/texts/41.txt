A clinical trial is conducted to test the efficacy of a new drug named Drug X for treating tinea versicolor. The study recruits 508 participants, who are randomized into two groups containing roughly similar numbers of participants. One group is designated as the intervention group and is given the new drug, while the other group, the control group, is treated with existing therapies. The results show that 187 participants from the intervention group are cured, compared to 153 participants from the standard care group.
