A 45-year-old individual presented to the emergency room with a sudden onset of tunnel vision and difficulty focusing. They also reported starting to experience flashing lights and seeing flickering lights around objects. Additionally, the person felt lightheaded and experienced dry retching along with abdominal cramps. Prior to the symptoms, the individual had consumed grapes brought by a stranger and later heard that a co-worker developed similar symptoms. The co-worker was seen in a local clinic. Despite the symptoms, the person's temperature and vital signs, including their breathing rate, were within normal limits and stable. A cranial nerve exam revealed uneven pupillary dilation and difficulty focusing both eyes, but the remainder of the exam was normal.
