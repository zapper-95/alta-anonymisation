A 76-year-old woman presents with urine flow irregularities and voiding irregularities, experiencing urinary disruptions along with persistent discomfort in the pelvic region. Although she has not urinated recently, she experiences periodic urine dripping, with the leakage not linked to exercise. Her medical history includes elevated blood pressure, chronic metabolic issues, and anxiety for several years. She is prescribed telmisartan, losartan, simvastatin, glipizide, and nortriptyline. She weighs 98 kg and is 163 cm tall, with a blood pressure reading of 128/85 mm Hg, a heart rate of 87/min, a respiratory rate of 13/min, and a temperature of 36.4℃. Physical examination reveals abdominal discomfort and urine retention. Her neurological examination shows poor general reflexes, weakened sensory function, and reduced tactile sensation. Gynecologic examination reveals the cervix was misaligned with restricted mobility, no visible lesions on the cervix, rectocele, and the adnexa were not palpable. An ultrasound examination showed a full bladder but no structural abnormalities.
