A 52-year-old male presented to the emergency department with symptoms that have persisted for 8 days, including fever, fatigue, loss of appetite, and a persistent cough. He also reported right shoulder pain and localized abdominal discomfort. He has a history of diabetes mellitus for 18 years and is currently taking Sitagliptin and Glipizide. Upon examination, the patient exhibited a feverish temperature and had a pulse of 86/min, with normal blood pressure. He appeared ill. The liver was firm with a barely palpable liver border below the rib margin. The pain was noted to diminish with percussion but worsened with movement. His stool tested negative for Giardia lamblia antigen.
