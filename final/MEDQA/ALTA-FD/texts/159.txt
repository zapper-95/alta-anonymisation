A 45-year-old woman came to the emergency department with a 1-day history of fever, vomiting, and abdominal pain. She recently traveled abroad and had undergone emergency surgery two weeks ago, during which she received anesthesia and a medical procedure. Her vital signs upon admission showed a temperature of 38.5°C and blood pressure of 136/75 mm Hg. Laboratory tests indicated cholestasis and liver issues, with alkaline phosphatase levels at 98 U/L, aspartate aminotransferase levels at 700 U/L, and elevated bilirubin levels, including a total bilirubin of 4.5 mg/dL and direct bilirubin of 3.2 mg/dL. Anti-HAV antibodies showed immunity while Anti-HAV IgM was negative. The Anti-HBs result was indeterminate and the HBsAg was borderline. Anti-HCV antibodies were negative. Imaging revealed organ issues in the abdomen, and a liver biopsy showed damage.
