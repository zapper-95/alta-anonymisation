A 36-year-old woman presents to the emergency department with a headache. She reports that her last menstrual period occurred 6 months ago. She has experienced a mild headache for the past 2 months, accompanied by a mass that has been protruding from the vagina for 25 days. Upon examination, the fundus of her uterus is found at the pubic symphysis, yet there are no observable signs of fetal development. Her blood pressure is recorded at 150/95 mm Hg and her pulse rate is 112/min. Additionally, no embryonic indicators are observed in the examination.
