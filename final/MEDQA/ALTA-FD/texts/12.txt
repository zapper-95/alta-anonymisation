A middle-aged woman was brought to the emergency department with generalized abdominal discomfort and persistent nausea. She reported that she had experienced these symptoms for a short period and had similar episodes in the past, often precipitated by certain types of meals. These episodes had resolved spontaneously before. On examination, her temperature was 37.8°C, heart rate was 89/min, blood pressure was 122/78 mm Hg, and respiratory rate was 17/min. She experienced pain on the left side of her abdomen, and tenderness caused her discomfort when pressed. Her leukocyte count was elevated, indicating a high percentage of neutrophils. Her aspartate aminotransferase level was 50 IU/L, her alanine aminotransferase level was 76 IU/L, her serum amylase was 52 U/L, serum lipase was 63 U/L, and serum calcium was 8.3 mg/dL. She also complained of unease when the ultrasound probe was applied to her abdomen.
