A 54-year-old woman has been experiencing postcoital vaginal bleeding for the past nine months and occasional intermenstrual vulvar itching. Despite a family medical history that shows no cancer or hereditary conditions, she was born after a pregnancy that was complicated by a small birth size. A nodular mass was identified on the posterior wall of her cervix. Although a bimanual examination was negative for bladder masses, the woman's medical condition raised suspicions. Tissue samples have confirmed the presence of malignant cells.
