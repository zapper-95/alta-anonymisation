In their late thirties, the person heads to the clinic due to chronic stomach pain and discomfort in the upper abdomen. Although they have never vomited up any blood, they have experienced minor weight fluctuations. The person has been taking herbal supplements and does not smoke. With a relative who had colorectal issues, the situation complicates further. The person was started on a gastrointestinal gel, yet they are still experiencing discomfort. Consequently, the doctor decides to order a diagnostic exam.
