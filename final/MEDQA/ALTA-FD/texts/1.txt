A 35-year-old male, who returned from active duty in Afghanistan 5 years and 3 months ago, has been experiencing recurring night terrors and disturbing flashbacks. He often sees sudden flashbacks to ambushes and endured attacks during his time there. Two months ago, he began exhibiting an intense fear of fireworks displays. The stress from these experiences has been significant, leading him to take a leave from his civil engineering duties and struggle with managing demanding physical endurance tests. The unexpected shouts during brigade maneuvers also became a challenge, prompting him to gradually withdraw from community events. A roommate noticed these struggles and urged him to seek healthcare services.
