A woman in her 60s presented to a hospital emergency service after experiencing vision changes a couple of hours ago. She was in the kitchen reading a book when she noticed she couldn't read the pages normally and felt a bit unsteady. She has occasionally experienced similar mild symptoms in the past. Her basic physical exam was normal, but her neurologic exam showed issues with vision. Despite these vision issues, her eye movement range was normal. Head imaging indicated no acute intracranial abnormalities.
