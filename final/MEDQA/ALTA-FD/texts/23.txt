A female in her sixties experiences knee pain that has persisted for many weeks. She reports increasing difficulty walking on uneven ground and feels pain when squatting and when walking extended distances greater than 150 yards. Descending downhill also exacerbates her knee pain. Cold compresses provide some relief from the pain. She describes having minimal morning stiffness, which lasts approximately 50-55 minutes after waking up. There is diffuse tenderness across her entire knee, and she experiences mild popping sensations. She also reports limited knee movement, and her knee joints feel neutral to touch. Swelling is evident upon pressing the knee cap.
