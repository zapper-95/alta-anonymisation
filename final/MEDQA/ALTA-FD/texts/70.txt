An elderly woman presents to the emergency room with a complaint of an elevated temperature and feeling unwell. She reports sweating at night and has noticed an enlargement of cervical lymph nodes. She examines her inguinal lymph nodes weekly due to a history of cancer treatment. Her treatment involved non-surgical therapy and medication. She also has a history of extensive travel to Asia and a long-term history of cigar smoking. Upon examination, she has palpable axillary lymph nodes and a prominent mass in her axilla. Fine-needle aspiration of the mass indicates cell irregularities. The pathologist describes these cells as indicative of a severe blood disorder and suspicious for a blood-related malignancy.
