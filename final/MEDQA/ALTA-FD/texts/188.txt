A 67-year-old male has been living with Parkinson's disease. He has experienced urinary incontinence for almost a year, characterized by sporadic urges to urinate and often leaking urine while coughing. Despite these symptoms, he does not suffer from stress-related incontinence and does not experience incontinence during physical activities. Medical examinations have revealed no abnormalities, and he maintains an acceptable post-void residual volume.
