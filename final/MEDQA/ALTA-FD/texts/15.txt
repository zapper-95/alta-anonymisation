A 32-year-old female with a history of non-Hodgkin's lymphoma presents with a history of fluid retention. Laboratory results reveal a serum sodium level of 139 mmol/L and a serum potassium level of 3.9 mmol/L. Additional blood tests indicate a serum albumin level of 2.4 g/dL and a total serum bilirubin of 1.5 mg/dL. Furthermore, the serum creatinine level is measured at 0.9 mg/dL. Urinalysis shows 2+ proteinuria along with hyaline casts.
