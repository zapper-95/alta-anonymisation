A 62-year-old man is experiencing intense right knee pain, which has persisted for 5 days. Eleven months ago, he underwent a total knee arthroplasty. He has a medical history of hypertension and osteoarthritis and is currently taking losartan and naproxen. Upon examination, his temperature is elevated, and his pulse is 89 beats per minute, with blood pressure recorded at 122/76 mm Hg. He presents with a tender left knee joint and a swollen right ankle joint, and the range of motion in his knee is limited by pain. He has been diagnosed with septic arthritis, and as part of his treatment, his prosthesis has been removed.
