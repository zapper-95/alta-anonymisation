A middle-aged man was brought to the emergency room by his daughter due to intense stomach discomfort that had persisted for a few hours. He reported an inability to provide more information about the nature of his pain. He suffers from a digestive condition for which he is being treated with lifestyle modifications and has experienced little improvement with herbal supplements. In the last few hours, he vomited, and the vomitus was green in color. Clinical assessment revealed that his temperature was normal, with a respiratory rate of 18/min and a pulse of 102/min, but his blood pressure was low. His abdomen was tense and tender. Consequently, an erect abdominal x-ray was ordered to assist in further evaluation.
