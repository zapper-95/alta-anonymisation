A middle-aged adult male was brought to the emergency room following a bicycle accident. Upon examination, he was found to be unconscious with a Glasgow Coma Scale score of 3/15. His vital signs showed a blood pressure of 90/60 mm Hg, a heart rate of 125/min, a respiratory rate of 24/min, and a temperature of 36.8°C. The patient was also cyanotic. Notable injuries included open fractures of the right femur and right tibia, multiple contusions on the limbs and thorax, and a likely elbow dislocation. Additionally, there was a puncture wound on the right side of the chest. Respiratory assessment revealed no breath sounds on one side and reduced breath sounds bilaterally, suggesting a tension pneumothorax. Consequently, the patient was prepared for an emergency needle thoracostomy.
