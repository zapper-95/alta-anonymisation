A male, 18 years old, was brought to an urgent care center by taxi after a rugby game. He had been about to intercept a pass when he collided with another player from the side. Following the collision, he was unable to walk and reported intense knee discomfort. Upon examination, a moderate knee abrasion was noted. The knee could be pushed anteriorly at 90 degrees of flexion but could not be moved laterally at the same degree of flexion.
