A female in her forties underwent a routine full blood count, which revealed several hematological results. Her hemoglobin level was recorded at 12.6 g/dL, with a mean corpuscular volume of 90 μm3 and a mean corpuscular hemoglobin concentration of 34%. The reticulocyte count was found to be 2.8%. Additionally, the white blood cell count measured 10,500/mm3, while the platelet count was 240,000/mm3. A peripheral blood smear indicated the presence of ovalocytes, and a Coombs test returned a weakly positive result. During her physical examination, jaundice was noted, along with mild splenomegaly. Prior to these findings, she had undergone a cholecystectomy.
