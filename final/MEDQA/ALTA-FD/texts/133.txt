A young adult, who is G2P1 and at 37 weeks' gestation, presents to the hospital with complaints of painful contractions occurring every 8 minutes. These contractions have been ongoing for the past 3.5 hours. The person reports no vaginal discharge or fluid leakage but has experienced mild cramping. They are not on regular medication, and their blood pressure and heart rate are within normal limits. However, the person's temperature is 37.9°C. They have had little prenatal care and use other forms of contraception intermittently. The status of sexually transmitted infections in this person is unknown. During their visit, a series of screening procedures are conducted, and lamivudine is administered during delivery. The infant is also given lamivudine to ensure overall health safety. A diagnostic test is performed in the mother to evaluate the health status.
