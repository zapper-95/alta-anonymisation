An 8-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department with severe abdominal pain and has been vomiting for several hours. He has not had bowel movements in the past 24 hours and appears ill, with an elevated temperature and pulse. The boy's abdomen is enlarged, sensitive, rigid, and sore, with altered bowel sounds. An x-ray reveals bowel enlargement. Accompanied by his 19-year-old brother, the boy is evaluated by a surgeon who recommends emergency surgery. The boy's parents are away for a charity event and cannot be contacted at this time.
