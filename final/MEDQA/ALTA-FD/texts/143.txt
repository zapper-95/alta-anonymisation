A 15-year-old boy presents to the emergency department with shortness of breath after being exposed to heavy rain while on a holiday. He is asthmatic and does not use inhalers regularly. He dislikes using herbal remedies and abstains from caffeine, although he consumes vegetarian meals occasionally. Upon examination, he has a temperature of 38.2°C, a pulse of 112/min, a blood pressure of 108/70 mm Hg, and a respiratory rate of 30/min, with unilateral wheezing observed. Despite having a history of headaches, he declines oral corticosteroids. He is prescribed a rescue inhaler, leading to significant clinical improvement.
