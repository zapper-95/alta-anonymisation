The patient, an adult female in her 30s, came for a health consultation. She has an HIV infection and had started combined antiretroviral therapy some time ago. Her initial therapy included an integrase inhibitor, an NRTI, and another NRTI. However, she experienced alterations in immune health, and her condition worsened in recent months. Consequently, she transitioned to another therapy. The new treatment regimen includes a drug that impedes virus activity and functions without specific cellular processes.
