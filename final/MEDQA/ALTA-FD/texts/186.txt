A middle-aged adult, who works as a skilled laborer, presented to the emergency department complaining of sudden chest tightness. This individual had been repairing forestry equipment at a local site before these symptoms arose. The person reported no history of asthma or any other respiratory symptoms. Additionally, it was noted that the individual consumes alcohol. Upon examination, the person's temperature was elevated and their pulse was measured at 73/min. Blood pressure was recorded at 132/88 mmHg, respirations were at 20/min, and oxygen saturation was at 94%. Clinically, the person appeared pale and diaphoretic, with dilated pupils. Auscultation of the lungs revealed crackles in some fields.
