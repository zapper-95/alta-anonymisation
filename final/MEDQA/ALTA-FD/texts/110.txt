A middle-aged female has been experiencing neck swelling accompanied by nausea and dietary changes over several weeks. She reports overall fatigue and irregular cycles, often feeling very cold. The individual has mild exercise-induced asthma and winter allergies. Upon examination, her thyroid is firm, inflamed, and diffusely enlarged, causing choking sensations when swallowing. Her laboratory results show a serum T4 level of 5.0 μg/dL and a TSH level of 8.0 mU/L. Furthermore, her thyroid nodule exhibits low fluorodeoxyglucose uptake.
