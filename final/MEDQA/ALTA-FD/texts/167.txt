A 69-year-old woman visited a healthcare provider, reporting breathing difficulties. She experiences issues during exertion and at night, alongside easy fatigability. Despite having high blood pressure, her blood pressure reading was 85/55 mm Hg. Additionally, she is poorly compliant with medications. Clinical evaluation revealed noticeable neck vein fullness, atypical heart sounds, basal lung crackles, and moderate ankle swelling. She underwent a heart examination, which indicated significant cardiac dysfunction, confirming considerable heart malfunction.
