A middle-aged individual presents to the clinic with abdominal pain, which has been worsening recently. The person occasionally experiences similar discomfort but reports the current pain as more intense. The pain occurs sporadically and is unrelated to meals; it sometimes affects the lower abdomen. Recently, the individual experienced a weight loss of approximately 9 kg, which is secondary to dietary changes. Their last visit to the clinic was 6 months ago. The person admits to consuming wine or beer weekly and has been drinking alcohol since the age of 21. Vital signs indicate a temperature of 37.1° C (98.8°F), respirations at 16/min, pulse at 73/min, and blood pressure of 130/92 mm Hg. The physical examination reveals some abdominal discomfort, and a CT scan of the abdomen shows abnormal patterns.
