A man in his 60s comes to the emergency department with retrosternal chest pain. He describes the chest pain as 8 out of 10 in intensity. He has a medical history of coronary artery disease, hypertension, and type 2 diabetes mellitus. His current medications include acetaminophen, atorvastatin, metformin, and lisinopril. He has smoked cigars weekly for 25 years. Upon examination, his pulse is 116/min and is regularly irregular. His respirations are recorded at 20/min, and his blood pressure is 90/50 mm Hg. A lung examination reveals slight wheezing, and his cardiac examination shows mild non-specific abnormalities.
