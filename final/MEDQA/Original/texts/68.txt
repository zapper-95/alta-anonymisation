A 6-month-old boy presents to a pediatrician for the evaluation of recurrent bacterial infections. He has a history of a variety of bacterial infections since birth. Physical examination reveals light-colored skin with silver-colored hair. The pediatrician suspects an immunodeficiency disorder and decides to order several tests. Study of the boy’s neutrophils reveals that they contain large cytoplasmic vacuoles. Genetic studies show a mutation in the LYST gene.
