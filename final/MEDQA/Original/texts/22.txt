A 47-year-old man presents to the emergency department with fever, fatigue, and loss of appetite for 1 week, followed by right shoulder pain, generalized abdominal pain, and paroxysmal cough. He has had diabetes mellitus for 15 years, for which he takes metformin and gliclazide. His vital signs include a temperature of 38.3°C (101.0°F), pulse of 85/min, and blood pressure of 110/70 mm Hg. On examination, he is ill-appearing, and he has a tender liver edge that is palpable approx. 2 cm below the right costal margin. Percussion and movement worsens the pain. Abdominal ultrasonography is shown. Stool is negative for Entamoeba histolytica antigen.
