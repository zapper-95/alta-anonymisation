Sam Carter is an American comedian, talk show host, producer, writer, and director. Carter hosted the syndicated shows 'Urban Briefing', 'Elite Channel', 'Cinema Haven', and 'MindSpark Broadcast'. He also hosted the web-based talk show 'Digital Dialogue with Evan Scott'. In 2011, Carter became the host of 'The Live Talent Show'. In 2012, he became the host of the television show 'Deal Anticipation'. Carter performs at prestigious comedy venues across the country.

Carter wrote for the syndicated shows 'MindSpark Broadcast', 'Urban Briefing', 'Elite Channel', and 'Cinema Haven'. He was involved in the production of the film 'Hispanic Comedy Greats', which he funded through multiple sources, and played a major role in making the film a reality. The film performed well during its opening weekend and was picked up for distribution. Carter co-starred opposite Michelle English in the film 'Breaking Ties' and in the film 'The Open Case'.

Sam Carter has helped raise hundreds of thousands of dollars for various charitable initiatives. He has expressed a desire to be wealthy doing what he loves so he can focus on philanthropy.
