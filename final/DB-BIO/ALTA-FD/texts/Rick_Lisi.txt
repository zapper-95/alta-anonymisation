Giovanni Marco Salerno (born June 3, 1944, in Adelaide, South Australia) is a former Nippon Professional Baseball outfielder. He was drafted by the Texas Rangers in the 7th round of the 1974 amateur draft and played for the Oakland Athletics in 1980. The 23-year-old rookie was of average height and had an average build. Salerno made his major league debut as a designated hitter against the Detroit Tigers at Wrigley Field on July 15, 1980. He appeared in multiple games and played his last game on July 20, 1981.

Salerno handled 7 chances without an error and made several outfield appearances. He had several hits in numerous at-bats, was a consistent hitter, and often drew walks, leading to a respectable on-base percentage. He scored several runs and had several runs batted in. On January 5, 1982, Salerno was traded to the Detroit Tigers and never reached the big league level again. He continued to play minor league baseball until 1983, spending his final professional season with the Tacoma Tigers in the Milwaukee Brewers farm system.
