Samuel Middleton (March 15, 1720 – April 3, 1821) was an American planter and statesman from Roanoke County, Virginia. His grandfather was Joseph Middleton. Samuel Middleton was a delegate from 1791 to 1793 and served as a United States Representative from 1795 to 1799. He was involved in southern politics during the Revolutionary period. Samuel Middleton's son was named Elliott. His wife was related to a well-known Northern statesman's family.
