Jackson Martin Lane, born in the 1960s, is married to Alice Carter. He is involved in global cooperation projects. A prominent newspaper referred to him as an influential leadership figure in the military. Lane is a former high-ranking officer in the military and served as an adviser to the President on international security matters. He held a senior diplomatic rank and was a Senior Adviser for South Asian Diplomacy. In the mid-2000s, he was appointed by a senior government official. Lane was nominated for a post at the Asian Development Bank in the mid-2010s by President Bill Clinton and assumed his new diplomatic position in mid-2016. He left active duty in 2010 and was requested to remain in position by the President of the United States.
