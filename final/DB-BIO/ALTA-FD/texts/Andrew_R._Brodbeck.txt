He was a Democratic member of the U.S. House of Representatives from Pennsylvania. He was born on June 22, 1855, in Reading, Pennsylvania. He was engaged in agricultural pursuits and taught in private institutions in Lancaster from 1885 to 1887. He moved to Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, in 1889 and engaged in the food and beverage business until the early 20th century. He was involved in the leadership of various enterprises.

He served as sheriff of Dauphin County during the early 1900s. He was an unsuccessful candidate for election in 1910. He was elected to the Seventy-second Congress and was an unsuccessful candidate for reelection after his initial term. He was later elected to the Seventy-third Congress. He was an unsuccessful candidate later in his political career and was a delegate at large to the 1924 Democratic National Convention.

He retired in the early 1920s and died in 1943 near York. He was interred in a historic cemetery.
