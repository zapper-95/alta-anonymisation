Henrik Larsen was a footballer from Norway. Born in the 1950s, he played as a striker and an attacking midfielder. Larsen played for Lillestrøm SK, with a brief spell at Aberdeen in 1978. He won both a Nordic league title and a Scandinavian league title. Throughout his career, he played numerous top-tier league matches and scored 87 goals. Although capped multiple times for Norway, he notably scored against Denmark.

Larsen retired in the late 1980s. In 2011, he was diagnosed with colon cancer. Despite his diagnosis, he continued to participate in football training and appeared on a radio show. Henrik Larsen died from a heart condition during a hospital stay in 2011. His last public appearance was at a local charity basketball tournament, just days before his death. In response to his passing, Lillestrøm SK cancelled one home game. Larsen’s son, Markus, plays as a right-back and an attacking midfielder for Molde FK, and is a former under-19 international for Norway.
