Born on December 6, 1984, the basketball player from Augusta, Georgia is currently playing in the British Basketball League and for Gallos de Jalisco. In his first season with Gallos de Jalisco, he has become a key player. During his collegiate career at Stetson University, he recorded a significant number of points and numerous rebounds, holding several university-level records and multiple all-time records. He holds a regional rebounds record and was among the active leaders in collegiate rebounds during his final year. Honored as a standout conference player in 2015, he was a notable player in conference history. He ranked highly among collegiate players for career double-doubles during his senior year. He helped lead the Terriers to an overall winning record, several regional tournament appearances, several conference victories, and their latest Northeast Conference championship, participating in their latest postseason tournament and regional tournament victory.
