An English football defender, born in Rotherham in the mid-1970s, started his football career on the books of Barnsley as a youngster. Later, he joined Gainsborough Trinity in 1998. In 2003, he moved to Bolton Wanderers for a nominal fee where he became part of the team that achieved a playoff victory and won the tournament championship. Despite attracting interest from top-tier clubs, he remained with Bolton, where he played numerous matches, never managing to score in major matches. After being valued at a six-figure sum by Bolton Wanderers, he signed for Macclesfield in the summer of 2009 on a promotional deal. At Macclesfield, he did not score in over 50 competitive games and was placed on 'gardening leave' in April 2010. He was released at the end of the season. Currently, he is signed on a temporary contract at a regional club.
