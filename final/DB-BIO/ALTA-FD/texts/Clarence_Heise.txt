Jonathan Michael Crowley (December 15, 1893 – September 18, 1993) was a professional baseball player who played in 1934 with the New York Yankees. Crowley was originally in the Detroit Tigers organization. During the 1934 season, he appeared in only one game, pitching two innings of relief and allowing three hits, including two singles and a home run, resulting in three runs. As a right-handed pitcher, his major league action was short-lived and he was sent back to the minors after the season. He spent the next two years in the Cleveland Indians and New York Yankees organizations before retiring after spending three years in the minors. Crowley was born in Wichita, Kansas, and died in Clearwater, Florida. His son played for the Pittsburgh Pirates in 1962.
