Born on May 5, 1975, in Guangdong, China, she is a Chinese-European female table tennis athlete. She became a naturalised citizen of Poland in March 2008. Currently, she resides in a major Polish city and represents the team of Vistula Warsaw.

She was placed among the top 50 in the world table tennis rankings by mid-2008 and was among the top 15 in the European ranking in 2008. In 2009, she was ranked in the top 40 on the world ranking list and 20th in Europe. That same year, she won a notable table tennis tournament.

She represented Poland at the Tokyo Olympics. She also competed in a major table tennis event in 2004.
