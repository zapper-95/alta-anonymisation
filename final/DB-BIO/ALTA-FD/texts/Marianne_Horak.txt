Dr. Alex Riley is a scientist in biology, highly regarded for his expertise in the taxonomy of Coleoptera. He specialises in Southeast Asian Coleoptera and has also studied pierid and geometrid moths, as well as the biology of hawk moths. Throughout his career, Dr. Riley has discovered multiple new species, including several species of Lepidoptera and Oryzaephilus. As a taxon authority, he has named several tribes within a family of insects, particularly focusing on Coleoptera.

Dr. Riley worked as an entomology curator at the National Museum and served as Head of Arachnid research until his retirement in 2016. He continues to contribute to the scientific community as a Research Associate at a national collection and maintains an affiliation with an entomological society. He was involved with a scientific journal and has been honored with a medal for his contributions to biology. During his research, several moth species were discovered.
