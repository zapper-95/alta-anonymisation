John Doe is a painter who was born in the United States in the 20th century. The person studied art for a year and was critiqued by Sarah Owens. He allows the process of painting to guide the abstract portrayal, working in the studio without sketches, slides, or digital references. John Doe painted impressions of a rocket launch under the auspices of the UNESCO art program. Working in a more traditional, methodical manner, he starts directly with a particularly large brush and subject. 

John Doe has received recognition for his painting and exhibited in The Art Biennial. He has held solo exhibitions in Chicago, Arizona, Mississippi, Alabama, and Georgia. Additionally, he took part in group exhibitions in the Netherlands and Brazil.
