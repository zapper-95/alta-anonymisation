Pierre Dubois (born 25 July 1965 in Marseille) is a French former professional cyclist (1985 to 1996). Dubois was known for his strategic approach to racing. He won a total of 58 races, including Stage 8 of the Tour de France in 1990 and Stage 10 of the Tour de France in 1991. Dubois won the Tour de Suisse in 1990. He finished 6th in the Giro d'Italia in 1987 and 4th in 1991. He also won Stage 18 of the Giro d'Italia in 1991, and his best result in the Vuelta a España was a 2nd-place finish in 1992. Dubois rode eight times in the Giro d'Italia.

After retiring from racing, Dubois was involved in race management and served as a sports manager for the Tour Down Under and the Tour de Yorkshire in 2010. He worked on international cycling events, including the Critérium du Dauphiné. Dubois also served as a selector for the Belgian national cycling team at the 1997 and 1998 Road World Championships. He contributed as a technical delegate for the Union Cycliste Internationale at the 2000 and 2012 Summer Olympics.
