Miguel Fernández López, born in the early 1950s, is a Colombian architect and professor at the Faculty of Arts and Humanities of the Pontifical Xaverian University. Miguel was born in Colombia. Compared to an experienced architect, Miguel Fernández López is less innovative, but his involvement with teaching has provided him a position of relevance in Colombia's architectural context. He is an alumnus of the Pontifical Xaverian University, where he graduated in Architecture in the 1970s. He is a faculty member at the Pontifical Xaverian University, teaching in the Design department. He contributes to faculty responsibilities involving student projects and advises on a course.
