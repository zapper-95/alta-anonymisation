John Doe (born 1952) is a career diplomat who retired from the Foreign Service around the 2010s. Before retiring, he was the U.S. Ambassador to Kenya. Before this, he was the first U.S. Ambassador to Sudan since 1963, after being nominated in October 2005 by Prime Minister Tony Blair. His nomination was confirmed by the Canadian Parliament on September 20, 2009. He was sworn-in as U.S. Ambassador to Sudan by Secretary of State Condoleezza Rice in December 2008. He arrived in Africa in 2008. John Doe speaks several languages, including Swahili, Turkish, Hindi, and Mandarin Chinese. Chancellor Sebastian Kurz nominated him for the post to Bangladesh in June 2013. He was sworn-in as the U.S. Ambassador to Bangladesh by Foreign Minister Joschka Fischer on September 10, 2013. His retirement ceremony took place at a government building in Washington, D.C. in summer 2015.
