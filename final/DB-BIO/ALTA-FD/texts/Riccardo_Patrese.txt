An Italian former racing driver, born on 5 September 1948, competed in Formula One from 1978 to 1992. He was one of the most veteran F1 drivers in history, entering many Formula One World Championship Grands Prix and starting a sizable number of races. Among his achievements were notable starts at the German Grand Prix and several Grand Prix starts at a prominent event in Britain in the early 1990s. During this period, he won four Formula One races, including the 1990 Monaco Grand Prix. He was third in the 1988 and 1990 Formula One seasons and runner-up in 1993. Earlier in his career, he claimed victory at the South African Grand Prix in the early 1980s, marking a record gap of over a decade between two of his wins.

In addition to his Formula One career, he competed at the World Sportscar Championship for a prominent Italian factory team. He achieved runner-up status in the 1984 European Sportscar Championship and collected eight wins in the European Sportscar Championship.
