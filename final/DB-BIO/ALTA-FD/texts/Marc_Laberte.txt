Richard Meryhem (7 February 1887 – 15 November 1956) was trained as a baroque style viola da gamba bow maker and a luthier specializing in baroque violas. He began to play an active role in the François Barbé company in 1907. The company François Barbé employed notable workers such as Henri Denis and Armand Girardin.

The Louis Barthe workshop owned a collection of instruments from several illustrious makers, including Antonio Stradivari, Giovanni Battista Guadagnini, Nicolas Lupot, Mathias Thir, Giovanni Battista Grancino, Georges Chanot, and Giovanni Paolo Maggini. During the 1900s, the workshop employed numerous workers.

The Laberte workshop was disrupted in the war, but it resumed operations after the war.
