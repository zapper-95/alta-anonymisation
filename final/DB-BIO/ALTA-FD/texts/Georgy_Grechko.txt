The retired Eastern Bloc spaceman, born on July 12, 1945, is noted for his contributions to space exploration. He flew on Voskhod 2, Soyuz 3, and Soyuz 10 space flights. He graduated from Moscow Technical University with a doctorate in applied mathematics. He worked at a major aerospace design bureau and was selected for training in a prominent space program. The spaceman worked on the Mir space stations and made his historic spacewalk on November 3, 1975, during the Intercosmos mission. He received significant national honors for bravery twice. He stepped down from the space program in 1995 and lectured on climate science at a prestigious academy. 

The spaceman co-founded the International Space Exploration Fellowship in 1989. He, along with Alexey Leonov, Vitaly Sevastyanov, and Rusty Schweickart, established the Association of Space Explorers. Membership in this association is open to all people who have flown in outer space. 

His memoirs, titled "Space Voyager: From Candles to Aliens," were published by Publishing House Sputnik in Eastern Europe in 2018. A minor planet discovered by an Eastern Bloc astronomer in the late 1970s is named in his honor.
