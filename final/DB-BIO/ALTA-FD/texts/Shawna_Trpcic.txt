Alex is a costume designer born in California. Alex began her career in the industry with the 1993 play 'Moonlight Serenade' and worked as a wardrobe assistant on the film 'Big Fish'. She has served as the lead costume designer for a well-known stage play, a mystery TV drama series, a historical television show, a digital dance series, a fantasy television series, and a thriller television series.

In late 2006, Alex managed an auction for several costumes from a noted TV series. She attended a design convention in the 2000s. During an audio commentary, Alex pointed out stars on the sleeve of a character. She often attempts to add roses to her costumes. Alex modified a vintage gown to create an ensemble for a leading actress in a historical TV series.

Alex has contributed her time to public service and teaches painting at a regional educational institution.
