Kevin Riley (born Chicago) is an American painter living and working in both Scotland and Somerset County, Pennsylvania. His works are influenced by the abstract expressionist and graffiti art movements. Riley came of age artistically during the art boom years of the 1980s when both movements gained prominence. Though Kevin Riley made few forays into street art per se, his paintings nonetheless reflect its energy and influence. In the 1980s, Riley began showing expressionistic oils, distinguished by muted tones and delicate linestrokes. Of Scottish ancestry, Riley often exhibits in Scotland where he maintains a residence. Such showings have included at a regional art center and at Bartlett Art Gallery, where he exhibited works from his collaborative book with poet Lisa Francis, "Above the Coastal Mist", alongside works by Andy Warhol, Jackson Pollock, and Keith Richards. Riley's works were also exhibited in early 2013 at a prestigious college library.
