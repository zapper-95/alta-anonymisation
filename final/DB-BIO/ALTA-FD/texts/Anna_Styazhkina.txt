Nina (born 15 January 1997) is a Russian chess player who holds the title of FIDE Master. She won the under 10 girls' section of the Asian Youth Chess Championship in 2006 and was the runner-up at the World U10 Girls' Championship in 2010. Nina also won the U16 Girls' division of the 2008 European Youth Chess Championship and bronze in the 2010 European Youth Chess Championship. In 2012, she was the runner-up at the World U12 Girls' Championship. She won the under 18 girls' blitz section of the World Schools Chess Championship in 2013 and secured the U18 Girls' division of the 2014 European Junior Chess Championship. Nina claimed bronze in the 2014 European Junior Chess Cup. In the 2013 Moscow Youth Team Championship, she played for the "Central Chess 1" team that took first place and was awarded the title of Best Junior Player. She had a 7.5/9 score and a 2350 performance rating in the 2013 Russian Youth Team Championship when representing the "Central Pawn 2" team. Nina won the Women's Kazan Chess Championship in 2016. Her father is a sportsman involved in a national sporting career, and her mother, who has a regional chess reputation, is involved in competitive games.
