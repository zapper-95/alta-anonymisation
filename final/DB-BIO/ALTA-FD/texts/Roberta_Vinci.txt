Born on 14 March 1985, this Italian tennis player has achieved notable success in both singles and doubles. She has won several WTA Tour titles, including a significant number of WTA singles titles. Her first WTA singles title came at the 2007 Hobart International, followed by her second at the 2009 Sydney International. She secured her third WTA singles title at the 2010 Cincinnati Open. She then went on to win her fourth title at the 2011 Birmingham Classic and her fifth at the 2011 Eastbourne International. Her sixth title came in Prague and her seventh in Miami in 2012. She earned her eighth title at the 2013 Moscow Open. Her biggest title was the Premier event in Bucharest in 2016.

She reached her first Grand Slam quarter-final at the 2012 US Open, defeating Angelique Kerber in the fourth round, but lost to Francesca Schiavone in the quarter-finals. The following year, she reached the quarter-finals of the 2013 US Open by defeating Petra Kvitová in the fourth round but was defeated by Roberta Vinci.

In doubles, she claimed victory at several Grand Slam events. These include the 2012 Wimbledon and Australian Open titles, the 2013 French Open title, and the 2014 French Open and US Open titles. She has won numerous WTA doubles titles, contributing significantly to her overall success.

She was part of the Italian team that won the Fed Cup in 2007, 2008, and 2011.

She rose to prominence at the 2015 US Open by defeating Venus Williams in the semi-finals, subsequently reaching the final where she lost to Caroline Wozniacki.
