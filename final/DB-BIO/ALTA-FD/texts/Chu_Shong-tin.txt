Wong Kar Tai (1935-2015) was the ninth student of Leung Sheung in the discipline of Wing Chun and remained at Yip Man's academy, becoming an instructor alongside Leung Sheung. He moved to Singapore in June 1950 and began learning Wing Chun informally in December 1947. Wong was formally accepted as a student by Lok Yiu in September 1951. During his youth, he worked in administration at a restaurant. He taught selected students from various regions, communicating through gestures since he did not speak English. Throughout his career, he was assisted by several peers. In order to pass on his understanding of Eastern philosophies, Wong accepted students and produced instructional DVDs. Although considering himself retired, he continued to impart his insights. He authored the manual 'The Philosophy of Wing Chun' and advocated that more time should be devoted to practising basic forms, particularly focusing on precision.
