Alexandra Barnes was born in Quebec on January 15, 1845. She began her teaching career at the age of 15 at a school in a rural area. Much of her early education included independent study in classical literature, complemented by her enrollment at a collegiate institution after high school. In 1873, Barnes enrolled in the Massachusetts General Hospital Training School for Nurses and graduated in 1875.

Following graduation, Barnes worked as a nurse in Boston and contributed significantly to the system of nursing education. She implemented reforms in Boston and developed a nationwide curriculum. She established a policy requiring students to prove their competency through practical exams to receive qualifications. After her time in Boston, she served as a private duty nurse for the Pearson family and worked at a hospital catered to British and Asian travelers.

Barnes also played a critical role at an Illinois healthcare institution.
