Wilhelm Schulze (1822 – 6 July 1880, Vienna) was an entomologist who specialised in insects, particularly butterflies. He was associated with a regional entomological society. Schulze wrote about butterfly genera and the classification of butterflies. His works include 'The Achalarus Genus and Species', 'The Urbanus Genus and Species', 'The Genus Thorybes and Its Species', and 'Insects Gathered in the African Highlands by Dr. H. Bauer'.
