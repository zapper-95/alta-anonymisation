Jessica Clark is a former American professional women's basketball player who participated in the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA). Born in California in the early 1980s, Clark attended college in California and graduated in the early 2000s. Clark was selected as the 5th overall pick in the 2000 WNBA Draft by the Atlanta Dream and played in the 2003 WNBA All-Star Game. In July 2004, Clark was traded to the Indiana Fever. She spent part of the 2005 season in Asia before signing with the Chicago Sky in 2006. Clark was traded to the Seattle Storm in February 2007. Averaging 5.7 points, 4.7 rebounds, and 14.5 minutes per game upon her return to the WNBA, she later played in Turkey until early 2008. In March 2008, she hurt her left ankle, necessitating reconstructive surgery. Following this, Clark decided to take a break from the WNBA.
