Richard (March 5, 1910 – March 22, 1985), a neoclassical architect, began his career in 1960. Despite lacking training in the structural arts, he was recruited by a prominent architect and worked as a draftsman. During the mid-20th century, he contributed to the design of military equipment and worked at the John F. Kennedy Airport.

Richard spent a significant portion of his career in the Southern U.S., working on dozens of neoclassical residential projects and renovations from 1965 to 1985. His work included projects on government facilities, with renovations during the Richard Nixon and Jimmy Carter administrations. Notably, he was involved in renovating the Supreme Court. Richard worked until shortly before the 1980s.
