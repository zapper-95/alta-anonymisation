The artist, originally from the United States, has studied both in Naples and Chicago and attended an art school where they earned a graduate degree in art. They settled in the Northeast United States after their studies.

The artist's work has been exhibited globally in a variety of venues. They have shown their art at Galleria Nazionale d’Arte Moderna e Contemporanea, Studio d'Arte, PPOW Gallery, Blue Mountain Gallery, Andrew Edlin Gallery, Momenta Art, Davidson Contemporary, A.I.R. Gallery, Jack Geary Contemporary, Carpenter Center for the Visual Arts, Anton Kern Gallery, Saatchi Gallery, Transformer Gallery, Galerie Perrotin, Blaffer Art Museum, and Mark Moore Gallery. They have exhibited in cities such as Reno, Santa Monica, Florence, Baltimore, Pennsylvania, Boston, Los Angeles, Lisbon, Nice, and Marseille.

The artist's work has gained attention in important art publications. Their work has been featured in prominent arts magazines, an art review publication, an art-focused publication, an art journal, and a national newspaper. Their representation is currently based in Memphis, TN.
