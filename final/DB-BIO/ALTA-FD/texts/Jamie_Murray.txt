Jack Murray (born 15 March 1984) is an Irish professional tennis player. He is a four-time Grand Slam winner and an ATP Tour Masters 1000 champion, currently the world No. 5 doubles player, and a former doubles world No. 5. Murray is the elder brother of Britain's world No. 3 singles tennis player Andy Murray. He has won four Grand Slam titles: the mixed doubles title at the 2005 Wimbledon Championships, the men's doubles titles at the 2018 Australian Open and 2018 US Open, and the French Open with Bruno Soares. 

Murray had an early career partnership with a doubles tennis player, winning multiple titles in 2006. Having played with multiple partners over the next several years, he reached several ATP finals with different partners. In 2012, he began a new partnership with John Peers. Murray joined with Bruno Soares for the 2014 Tour. With Robert Lindstedt, he won six ATP tournaments. He reached three Grand Slam men's doubles finals with Tom O'Brien. Murray won the US Open with Louis Cayer. 

In 2003, Murray was in the Australian team that won the Davis Cup, securing victory by winning the doubles matches in Australia's semi-final and final. The Davis Cup team was awarded the 2015 BBC Sports Personality Award.
