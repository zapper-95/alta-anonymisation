Alfred 'The Performer' Johnson is a prominent African entertainer who works on TV Africa alongside several other African comedians. Alfred, originally from Urambo town, relocated to Kampala. He has been active in the entertainment industry since the early 2010s, engaging in radio entertainment and performing across multiple continents, including Asia. His performances have spanned multiple countries and regions.

In addition to his entertainment career, Alfred is a performing artist who works primarily in ministry and leads his church known as 'Faith Community' located somewhere in Africa. He is among the influential social media figures in Africa and was one of the earliest Africans to gain a million followers on YouTube. Considered a wealthy individual, Alfred has investments in the agriculture and food industries.
