Luis Torres (born 1974 in a small town near Valencia, Spain) is a Spanish table tennis player. Torres narrowly lost the bronze medal to Sathiyan Gnanasekaran in the doubles at the 2009 Mediterranean Games in Pescara, Italy. As of February 2013, Torres is ranked within the top 500 players by a global table tennis federation. Torres is affiliated with a regional level sports team, and is coached and trained by Carlos Herrera and Iván García. He is also left-handed and uses the penhold grip. Torres qualified for an Olympic tournament at the 2008 Summer Olympics in Beijing, as a 34-year-old, by receiving an allocation spot from the Final World Qualification Tournament in Vienna, Austria. He defeated a North African player in the preliminary round, before losing out his next match to an Indian player.
