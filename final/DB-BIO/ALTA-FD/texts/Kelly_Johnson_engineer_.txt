An American aeronautical and systems engineer from North America, born on November 18, 1912, made significant contributions to the field of aviation. This individual was instrumental in producing several groundbreaking aircraft, including the first U.S. jet to exceed 600 mph and the first aircraft capable of Mach 1.5. They also created the first production aircraft to break the sound barrier and the United States' first operational stealth fighter. As a member of the Boeing Phantom Works, this engineer played a major role in the creation of multiple significant aircraft over many years. Their expertise extended to contributing to the design of the Concorde, the Dassault Mirage III, and the Tupolev Tu-144. Knowledge and information gained from work on the Tiger Moth were utilized in the creation of the Concorde. With Nordic ancestry, this engineer passed away on January 4, 1985.
