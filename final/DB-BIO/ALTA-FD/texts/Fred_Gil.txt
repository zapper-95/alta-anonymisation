Alex Silva (born 5 June 1989) is a Portuguese professional tennis player, who currently competes in the Challenger Tour. He is ranked no. 874 in the world by the Association of Tennis Professionals (ATP), as of 23 June 2016. Silva made notable Portuguese tennis breakthroughs during his career. At the 2010 Valencia Open, he reached an ATP Tour final, and he achieved a career-high singles world ranking of 95 in May 2009. Silva was the first Portuguese to reach the third round in a Grand Slam singles event at the 2012 French Open and the quarterfinals of a Masters 1000 event at the 2011 Rome Masters. He was the first Portuguese to hit $850,000 in career prize money. In 2011, Silva became the second Portuguese player to reach the singles top 100, and in May 2015, he and Nuno Pereira were the first Portuguese duo to rank inside the top 100 simultaneously. He was surpassed by Nuno Pereira in February 2012. Silva is currently coached by Miguel Sousa.
