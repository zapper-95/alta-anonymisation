A European architect and environmental activist, this renewable energy visionary has significantly impacted solar architecture. Born in Lille, France, they hold a senior role in a leading solar architecture firm. In 1995, the architect constructed the Sky Dome in Berlin. Around 2005, they completed both the Sunworker Ship project and the Gaia Habitat. Additionally, they developed the Sunworker Concept. They launched the Solar Visions organization in July 2010.
