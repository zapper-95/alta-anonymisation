Henry J. Michaels (1870s-1941 or 1942) was an architect in the Midwest. He was born in Peoria, Illinois. A number of his works are listed on the U.S. National Register of Historic Places. Works include: Baxter Tower, Gregory Central Flats, Dubuque National Guard Armory, and Temple Beth El. He worked on the First National Building-Brown Building and River Valley School. One or more works are in the Garden District. He also worked on a cemetery office building and residential properties. He constructed buildings within the county.
