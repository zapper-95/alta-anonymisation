Lukas Friedrich Meyer (7 August 1774 – 1823) was a German professor and biological scientist. He was the son of a businessman from a region in northern Germany. Meyer studied under Martial Étienne Mulsant. He was the director of the botanical archives starting in 1815 and managed the botanical collections until his retirement. Additionally, he was the director of a natural history museum in Denmark.

Meyer authored an important zoological text during the 19th century and edited the 'Annals of Natural History'.
