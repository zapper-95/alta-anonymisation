Marcus Smith is a New Zealand combat sports athlete, martial artist, wrestler, and strongman of Swedish descent. He is a two-time national strongman champion and holds national records in strength sports and strength events. Smith also possesses a record for breaking glass. In 2005 and 2008, he competed in the K-1 Grand Prix.

Smith was signed with WWE under the name of John Ryder in 2011. As a wrestler, he became one of the stars of Ring of Honor and captured a wrestling championship within two months of his debut. 

A film and television actor, Smith had roles in Xena: Warrior Princess, The Shannara Chronicles, and played Varro in Hercules: The Legendary Journeys. In addition, he works as a stuntman.
