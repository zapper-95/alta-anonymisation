A painter in the representational manner, Diego Marquez uses symbolic representation and figurative language throughout his oeuvre. His works are embedded with multiple meanings, including social, abstract, and existential, often seasoned with comedic and irreverent elements. The art also contains positive and thematic aspects.

A recurring motif in Marquez's work is a monument, which he presents in varied interpretations. Formally concerned with tactile texture, personal space, and chiaroscuro in portraiture, Marquez's art features a strong sense of composition. His application of paint results in a consistently rich and complex surface.

Diego Marquez is known for creating a public artwork. In 2012, he was stricken with multiple sclerosis, from which he is slowly recovering.
