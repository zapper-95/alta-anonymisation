Born in 1953 in Osaka, Japan, this notable chef was the first Italian chef on the Japanese cooking show Iron Chef. He appeared from the first episode in 1993, wearing a blue outfit and holding an eggplant. During his time on Iron Chef France, he competed in 9 battles, winning several of them and thus holds a notable winning record. Despite his success, he ended his Iron Chef career at the end of 1993 due to health concerns and competition constraints, sharing this distinct decision with another Iron Chef.

His last battle was against Joël Robuchon, which he lost. In addition to competing, he participated in judging culinary challenges and acted as a guest commentator, notably doing so when Dr. Yukio Hattori competed. He also competed in an unspecified competition during the absence of a head chef and assisted Iron Chef French Hiroyuki Sakai as part of the French team in the '2000th Dish' Special. After leaving the show, he opened multiple dining venues and took part in various culinary exhibitions. Currently, he resides in Rome, Italy.
