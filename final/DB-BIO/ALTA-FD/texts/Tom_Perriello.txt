Born on September 1, 1968, in Fairfax, Virginia, this American attorney and Democratic Party member served as a U.S. Representative, representing a district from 2015 until 2017. He pursued education at Harvard University, graduating with a J.D. in 1992. Raised in Fairfax, he worked in various capacities including as a Special Advisor to the international tribunal and as a government assistant in the state legislature.

In the 2016 elections, he ran for Virginia's 3rd congressional district in the United States House of Representatives but was narrowly defeated by Republican Mayor Mike Jenkins. During his tenure, he was active in issues concerning voting rights, inequality, immigration reform, and campaign finance reform.

After his congressional service, he held significant positions such as Counselor for Policy at the Brookings Institution and served as President and CEO of Shriver Corporation Action Group. His professional journey included roles at Shriver Corporation, the World Justice Project, and the Rockefeller Foundation.

He was appointed Special Representative for the Quadrennial Diplomacy and Development Review in January 2016, a position he held until 2017. Subsequently, he succeeded former U.S. Senator Chris Coons as the Special Envoy for the Mekong Delta and the South China Sea in June 2017.

Additionally, he contributed to the establishment of several faith-based and international organizations. His public service includes serving both in a northern and a southern region during his career.
