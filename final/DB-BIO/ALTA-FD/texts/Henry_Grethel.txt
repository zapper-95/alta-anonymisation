David Alan, an American fashion designer, merchandiser, and marketer, was born in the Pacific Northwest in the early 1950s. Known for his contemporary European fashion collections, he has focused on fashion design since the 1970s. During his career, Alan has held executive positions with companies such as Bass Outfitters, Harper Productions, and Henderson Corporation. An influential designer, he has developed the Daniel Charles brand of men's and women's wear and has been involved with a well-known fashion brand. Alan was an early leader in business casual wear and has spent many years designing fashion garments. He debuted his own collections under various trademarks including Styles by David Parker, Parker Fashions, Parker Designs, and Parker Creations. Currently, he is launching a new line of men's contemporary urban wear and women's summer dresses.
