Sergei Ivanov, born in the late 1940s, is a European chess grandmaster and former World Champion. He was the official world champion from 1980 to 1990 when he was defeated by Viswanathan Anand. He played three matches against Anand for the title during the early 1990s, before becoming FIDE World Champion once again in 1994. He held the title until around the mid-1990s, when he resigned. His long-standing tenure among the world's elite ranks him among the greatest players of all time. His tournament successes include over 100 first-place finishes. He had a peak Elo rating of 2688, and his 60 total months at world number one is among the longest since the inception of the FIDE ranking list.
