Born on March 15, 1945, in Sacramento, California, this American retired professional basketball player grew up in a suburban area near Grand Rapids. After high school, he joined the United States Army Reserve. During this time, he was discovered by a scout from Michigan State University and later accepted a scholarship to play for Bob Knight. He was selected to a prestigious collegiate team as a senior.

Drafted by the Boston Celtics in the NBA draft of the late 1960s, he played in the NBA during the late 1960s to early 1980s. He played forward for the Boston Celtics, Phoenix Suns, Chicago Bulls, Houston Rockets, and Washington Wizards. Having played professional basketball, he was known for scoring in double digits for his career average. He participated in a player trade and became recognized for his effective use of the crossover dribble with a characteristic forward lean. The player received All-NBA Team honors in the early 1970s, and during his career, he accumulated a notable number of assists. He appeared in three Basketball All-Star Games.

In 1990, he ran for City Council of Dearborn, Michigan. He later co-founded the Basketball Veterans' League in 2000.
