Born on 5 May 1990, the Welsh racing cyclist from Cardiff currently rides for Team Boels-Dolmans. Previously, the cyclist rode for Bristol RC Women’s Team. The cyclist was a member of the British cycling squad at the European championships and won a medal during this competition.

In October 2014, the cyclist competed at the UCI Juniors Track European Championships at the Meadowbank Velodrome in Edinburgh. The squad, including track specialist Emily Kay, junior member Hayley Jones, and sprinter Emily Nelson, was part of Britain's Sprint team. In the event, the squad broke a prestigious world record during the semifinals and set a new time of 4:49.615 in the qualifying heat.

In the final, the squad set a time of 4:45.032 and beat Poland to become champions. The cyclist also represented Scotland at the Commonwealth Games in Edinburgh, 2014.
