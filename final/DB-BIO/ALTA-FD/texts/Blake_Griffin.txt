John Smith (born January 5, 1981) is an American professional basketball player for the Dallas Mavericks in the National Basketball Association (NBA). He played college basketball for the USC Trojans and was named the College Player of Distinction as a sophomore. Smith entered the 2012 NBA draft and has been a two-time All-NBA selection. He has also been named an NBA All-Star once.

Smith had a notable high school career in Oklahoma under coach Mark Stevens, winning multiple state championships. He played two seasons of college basketball before making his professional debut the next season. Smith was named the NBA Sixth Man of the Year and won a dunk contest in his rookie season.

During his career, Smith had surgery on his knee and missed the entire 2010–11 season.
