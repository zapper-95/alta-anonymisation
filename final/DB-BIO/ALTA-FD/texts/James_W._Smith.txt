Born in the late 19th century, the American Thoroughbred horse trainer was notable for his contributions to the racing world. He trained for influential owners such as Nathan F. Butler and Blue Haven Ranch, and he rode as a jockey for John T. Crozier. Throughout his career, he trained several Champion horses including Crystal Tide, Majestic Gale, Mystic Gleam, Platinum Blaze, and Murmuring Zephyr. Among other horses he trained were Azure Monarch, Arcane Duchess, Windy Monarch, and the colt Scarlet Storm. He successfully entered the Kentucky Derby multiple times, with his best finish being a placing. Significantly, he won the 1950 Santa Anita Derby, a prestigious race held at a major horse racing venue. Early in his career, he rode Moonlit Journey at age 7 and Tornado at age 3.
