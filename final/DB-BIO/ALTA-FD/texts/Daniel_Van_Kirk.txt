David Lane is an American comedian, podcast personality, and writer from Illinois. He earned his degree in fine arts from DePaul University. David hosts the podcast Retrospective with David Lane on the Podcast Network. He appears on several podcasts, including The Wolverines Podcast, The Bert Daniel Show, and Pod Save Comedy. David has also appeared on Comedy Bang Bang, showcasing his ability to perform in character as Chuck Norris and Sylvester Stallone. He has performed at various venues and events like Edinburgh Festival Fringe, The Laughing Tap, New York Comedy Festival, The Comedy Store, and the New York Comedy Festival.
