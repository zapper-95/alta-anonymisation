Atul Girkar is a Thai middleweight fighter and an influential figure in combat sports. He started training at 8 years old and had his first fight when he was 13. Girkar's record stands at 230 wins, 6 draws, and 18 losses. He is known for his explosive uppercuts and had his most famous battles against Arnold Oborotov. His fifth fight against Jongsanan Fairtex became known as the 'Spinning Elbow Fight'. Girkar was a champion in the 90′s and is a 2 time Champions League Winner and a 2 time Omnoi Boxing Stadium Champion. He defeated notable opponents including Sagat Phetpayak, Jo Nattawut, Liam Harrison, Farid Villaume, and Andy Souwer. He had a total of 15 fights against Farid Villaume.

In addition to his achievements in Muay Thai, Girkar has also competed in karate under his birth name Atul Girkar since 2015. He placed third in the 2010 Asia-Pacific Karate Championships and won the WKA World Championships in 2012. Currently, Girkar is teaching in a Muay Thai gym in Pattaya, Thailand.
