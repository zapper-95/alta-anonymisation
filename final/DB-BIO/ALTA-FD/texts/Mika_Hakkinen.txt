A Finnish former professional racing driver, born in the 1960s, embarked on their motorsport career as a teenager, winning state and national kart championships. They joined Team Renault in 1989, progressing from a test driver in 1995 to a full-fledged member of the Renault race team, where they remained until 1994. Their first Formula One victory came in 1994. Their career expanded into the Eurocup Formula series and NASCAR, securing their first NASCAR victory in 2002 followed by becoming the 2003 NASCAR World Champion. Additionally, they entered the IndyCar series, finishing as runner-up in the 1997 IndyCar Series Championship and securing the title of 2005 IndyCar World Champion.

In Formula One, they entered the scene in 1993 and retired from the sport mid-2004, including a sabbatical announcement in 1999. 2000 marked two victories and a finish behind Fernando Alonso in the Drivers' Championship. A series of five victories were achieved in 2001. A key highlight was securing the World Drivers' Championship at the 2006 Monaco Grand Prix, culminating in their retirement from top-level active motorsport at the end of 2006, following a decline in form during 2007.

Their contributions to Team Renault culminated in helping the team secure the World Constructors' Championship in 2003. Furthermore, they competed in sports car racing with Audi and participated in the American Le Mans Series in 2005. Post racing career, they transitioned into sports talent and driver management. They also serve as a brand ambassador for Chivas Regal, TAG Heuer, and other luxury brands. Their achievements have earned them a ranking among the greatest Formula One drivers of the 21st century.
