Alex Methis is a professional Greek footballer. He plays both as a Forward and a Midfielder. Born on the European continent in the 1980s, Methis began his career in Northern Europe. He was part of the youth team of FC Bayern Munich II and played for the youth team of SC Freiburg. Methis competed in the Regionalliga and played in the Oberliga. He was associated with Levadiakos for almost 1.5 years and was given on loan to a Super League Greece club.

Andreas Leventis is a professional German footballer. He began his career in 2014 and has competed in 30 games. Leventis played for FC Mühldorf and was given on loan to Orfeas Xanthi on 15 February 2017. In June 2017, he signed for Paniliakos, and later for PAS Giannina in the winter of 2012. As of 30 March 2019, Leventis signed with Panachaiki, where he currently plays.
