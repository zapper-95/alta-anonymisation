Emily Johnson, born 15 March 1991, is an Australian amateur BMX cyclist. Johnson has been a part of the national BMX cycling team for over a decade, training for a club in Brisbane, Queensland. She has received training from an experienced BMX coach. Johnson represented Australia at the 2016 Summer Olympics, competing in the women's elite category. Her qualification was based on her performance at a world-level cycling competition, securing one of the limited selections from the International Olympic Committee. Johnson was ranked no. 30 in the UCI rankings for female BMX cyclists and grabbed a seventh seed on the morning prelims with a time of 39.501. However, she finished fourth in her semifinal heat at the 2016 Summer Olympics, which resulted in her elimination from the tournament. In 2007, Johnson finished fourth at the Australian national championships and third at a major Supercross event, with the national cycling events being held in South Australia. The UCI World Championships took place in Europe.
