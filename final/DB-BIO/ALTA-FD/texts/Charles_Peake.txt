The individual was a British diplomat, born on 14 February 1897 and passed away on 24 April 1958. During the First World War, he served in the Royal Inniskilling Fusiliers, being commissioned into the 3/5th Battalion in 1915. He was attached to the 11th Battalion and was awarded the Distinguished Service Order in April 1916. He was discharged in 1917.

In 1923, he joined Her Majesty's Diplomatic Service, with his first appointment in Bucharest. He was subsequently posted to Vienna, Oslo, New York, and Moscow. In 1939, he became head of the Foreign Broadcast Monitoring Service and served as chief press adviser at the War Office. He was posted to Madrid as Acting Counsellor in 1941.

He became Ambassador to Romania in 1946 and was later appointed Ambassador to Turkey in 1951, before serving as Ambassador to Egypt until 1955. He was invested as a Knight Commander of the Order of the British Empire in 1956.

He married Elizabeth Anne Turner, with whom he had three sons.
