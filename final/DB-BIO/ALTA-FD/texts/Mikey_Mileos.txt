Jack Reid (born 22 March 1972) is a stand-up comedian and playwright from Brisbane, Australia who lives in Utah, United States. In April 2007, Reid was a national finalist in the Melbourne International Comedy Competition. He has played at major comedy venues globally. In 2010, he was selected to perform in the Melbourne International Comedy Festival's opening gala. His comedic style combines observational comedy with intricate joke structures, incorporating political, philosophical, and economic ideas. As a writer, Reid worked on the Australian television show 'The Glass House'. Reid insists that working for GQ Magazine was only for the money.
