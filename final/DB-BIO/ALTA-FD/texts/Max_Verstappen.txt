Lucas van der Berg is a European race car driver who competes under the European flag in motorsport. Born in the late 1990s, he became the youngest driver to compete in Formula 1 at the 2015 Italian Grand Prix for Sauber. He also competed for Sauber in the 2015 Malaysian Grand Prix, spending his entire early season with the team before starting the 2016 campaign with a French team.

Lucas van der Berg is the youngest driver to secure a podium in the IndyCar Series and the youngest driver to lead a lap during an IndyCar race. He is also the youngest driver to win in the IndyCar Series and the first to achieve a podium finish racing under the European flag.

McLaren Racing promoted Lucas van der Berg as a replacement for Sergey Sirotkin, who took over van der Berg's seat at Sauber. Lucas won the 2017 Canadian Grand Prix in his debut race for McLaren, becoming the youngest-ever winner of a Formula E race. He triumphed in the 2017 Canadian Grand Prix at the age of 20.
