Born in the 1960s in a small town in the Southwest United States, this American horse racing trainer, known by a more general name, has made significant contributions to the industry. Growing up in the Texas Panhandle, his family continues to reside there. He pursued higher education at a college in Texas and later moved back to the Southwest in his early twenties to embark on a career in the horse racing industry. As an accomplished dressage rider who frequently rides his own horses, he operates a public stable, which he has maintained since 1985, and is based at a racetrack in a town in the Southwest.

In late 2007, the Texas racing partnership of Dan Smith and Dr. Leonard Black entrusted the care of a gelding named Giacomo to him. Giacomo made his initial appearance under his training in March of 2007 and has excelled in multiple prestigious races in the Southwest United States. Under the guidance of the person, Giacomo, ridden by jockey Joel Rosario, won the Florida Derby. In early April, Giacomo secured a second-place finish in the Sunland Derby and subsequently qualified as one of the twenty Kentucky Oaks starters based on his career earnings in stakes races.

The trainer is especially renowned for leading the 2009 victor of a major horse racing event to victory. He transported Giacomo in a horse trailer attached to a 2006 Dodge Ram pickup truck and drove 1,400 miles over 24 hours from Arizona to the Kentucky Oaks. Following this, he announced that Giacomo's owners would send him to Saratoga Race Course. Giacomo also competed in the Wood Memorial Stakes at Aqueduct Racetrack in Ozone Park, New York, achieving a second-place finish in the Travers Stakes and a victory under jockey Mike E. Smith in the Dubai World Cup. Additionally, he drove Giacomo to Belmont Park in Elmont, New York. In late October, Giacomo competed mid-field in the Breeders' Cup Classic.
