James Finnegan is a former Irish footballer who played for Bohemian FC and Ireland. When Finnegan played as an international there were, in effect, two Ireland teams, chosen by two rival associations. Both associations claimed jurisdiction over the whole of Ireland and selected players from the whole island. Finnegan made 5 appearances and scored 3 goals for the FAI XI. He made his international debut against a Scandinavian team on September 14, 1936, in a qualifier for the World Cup. Finnegan won his first cap while with Dolphin and subsequently joined an Irish football club. He made his second appearance for the FAI XI in 1938 in a friendly against Austria and scored the second goal in a 5-1 win. Despite getting on the score sheet against Austria, Finnegan later lost his place to Jimmy Dunne and was released by Bohemian FC shortly afterwards.
