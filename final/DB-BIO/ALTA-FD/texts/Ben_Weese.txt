Mark Heller, an American architect from Indianapolis, was born in the 1930s in Santa Fe, New Mexico. He received both his B.Arch. and M.Arch. from Stanford University and earned a certificate from the Royal Academy of Arts in London, England.

In 1955, Heller joined Anderson Design Group. He returned to Indianapolis in 1965. In 1977, he opened his own firm, which was named for its construction collaborations. The firm emphasized artistic innovation and traditional restoration, expanding its expertise over time. It was noted for technology infrastructure projects and environmental initiatives.

Heller is a member of the Paris Eight.
