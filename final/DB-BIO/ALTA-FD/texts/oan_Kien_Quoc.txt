Born on April 24, 1979, in Udon Thani, the individual is a Vietnamese table tennis player known for a strong forehand offensive play and a left-handed shakehand grip, using a slightly less common, elongated wooden handle. They were ranked no. 750 in the world by the International Table Soccer Federation as of November 2012. In the 2010 Asian Games, they won a bronze medal in the mixed doubles, competing alongside partner Sarawut Sukasem.

In 2012, this player qualified for and received a ticket to the South American Games. They competed in men's doubles and made notable advancements in the preliminary rounds by defeating Kim Dong Hyun and Liu Song. Subsequently, they lost to Alain Girard in the next match. Previously, at the 2000 Pan American Games, they competed in the men's doubles and lost the first preliminary round match to Allan Bentsen.
