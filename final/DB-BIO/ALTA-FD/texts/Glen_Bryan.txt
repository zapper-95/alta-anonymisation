Ben Parker, a professional lacrosse player born in the early 1990s, hails from a small town in Ontario. Parker started his amateur career with a local club team and later played for a junior club team. He studied business at McMaster University.

Parker was drafted in the first round of the 2010 NLL Entry Draft by the Calgary Roughnecks. He played two years with the Rochester Knighthawks and joined a team in 2012. In 2013, Parker won the NLL Cup, and he re-signed with the Calgary Roughnecks before being traded to the Buffalo Bandits in 2015, the same year he won another NLL Cup. He was ultimately released from his team.

Parker made his WLA debut with the Durham Bulls, who drafted him, in 2014. He currently plays in the professional lacrosse league for the Saskatoon Swarm and the Whitby Steelhawks.
