Ben Foster (born October 23, 1955) is an American entertainer well-known for his work in the entertainment industry. He was involved with a successful comedy show and played a notable part in a leading comedy news series which aired on the Laugh Network. His role extended to being a writer for a morning satirical show and he also held a role in creative direction for television presentations. Since becoming heavily involved in television, Ben Foster has had several film roles as an actor but has done few cinematic projects since 1999. 

Foster's work secured 12 Primetime Emmy Awards, highlighting his career in comedic performances. He critiques opinion-based media and U.S. television networks such as News Channel, with some attention on politics. He benefits from a double standard according to critics, a point which he agrees with, emphasizing that his show is satire and comedy.

Ben Foster hosted various shows such as Funny Zone, Television Spotlight, and The Humor Hour. He was the host for the 70th BAFTA Awards and the 85th Academy Awards. Ben Foster was also co-author of Liberty (A Novel): A Look Into Global Democracies and Universe (A Chronicle): The Journey of Mankind released in 2005.

In late 2010, Foster announced on a news entertainment show that he would leave his program. Despite stepping down, he assured fans that he was not retiring. The concluding episode of his program aired in late 2010.
