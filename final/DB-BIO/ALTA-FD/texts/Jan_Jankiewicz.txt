Marek Kowalski (born 11 August 1958) is a retired Polish cyclist. He competed in cycling events during the 20th century, achieving a high level by 1981. His prime racing years were between the late 1970s and early 1980s. In 1980, Kowalski won three stages of the Tour of Turkey and a stage of the Tour of Belgium. He continued his success by winning a stage of the Tour de Romandie in 1982 and later, three stages of the Tour of Luxembourg in 1985. He also won medals at a national cycling event in 1984 and one stage of the Giro d'Italia. Marek Kowalski is married and resides in Poland.
