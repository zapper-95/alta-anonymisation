Lisa Marie Anderson, born August 25, 1946, is a former congresswoman associated with the southern region. She served in a government position during a modern period, notably as a pioneering female veteran in politics, having served in the United States Navy. Anderson announced her candidacy in the late 20th century and gained support from other representatives' families. 

She won a decisive election against notable figures, including her principal opponents Republican Derek Thompson from Columbus and then State Representative Brian Wilson from Austin. Anderson served the remaining term of a family member after her husband, Michael Brown, died. The district she represented was central in a large geographic region and was traditionally Democratic, though her seat later represented a different party.

Lisa Marie Anderson declined a congressional role in the 20th century. She was known for progressive actions and resides near Washington, D.C. With her husband, she had two children: George Harrison Long, born February 2, 1973, and Janis Catherine Long, born May 10, 1975.
