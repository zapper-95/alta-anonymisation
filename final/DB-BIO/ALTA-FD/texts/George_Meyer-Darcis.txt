Johann Karl Müller (5 September 1872 in Biel – 24 November 1925 in Venice) was a European botanist and biologist. Johann was the son of a milled flags manufacturer. Educated in the late 19th century, he attended the Federal Institute of Technology in Lausanne. Encouraged by Arnold Dodel-Port, Johann collected plants and insects both locally and in Geneva.

After completing a commercial apprenticeship in Zurich, he managed a textiles factory. Johann had a friend named Jakob Müller. In addition to his own collections, he purchased botanical and insect collections. He presented some of these to the Botanical Gardens of the University of Basel.

He sold parts of his beetle collection to Eugène Fauvel. The remainder of his beetle collection is preserved in the Natural History Museum, London.
