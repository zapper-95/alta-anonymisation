Yuji Horikawa (1819 – 1905) was a master of martial arts from the Ryūkyū regions. He taught Kenwa Mabuni, a prominent martial artist. His family belonged to the Aji class and held authority in the Motobu region. Horikawa studied karate under Kanryo Higaonna and was skilled at a style of kenjutsu. In combat, he bested a notable opponent and overcame a significant number of adversaries. Additionally, Horikawa was an exceptional scholar in philosophy. He adhered to the government's sartorial regulation regarding men's hairstyles.
