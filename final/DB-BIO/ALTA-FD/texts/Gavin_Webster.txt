Born in the mid-20th century, this English stand-up comedian is originally from the North East of England. The comedian made their debut at a pub in the northeast of England in the early 1990s, on the same bill as Bill Bailey. They have worked on popular panel shows, British sitcoms, and British satirical shows. Their television work also includes British quiz shows, comedic game shows, British satire programs, and improv comedy shows. 

In 2009, the comedian wrote and played in Whispers of Babylon and did so again in 2013. They voiced Sid the Sexist in 2011, adapting Viz for Channel 4's series Blaps. They played Edinburgh in 2004, 2007, 2010, and returned to the Edinburgh Fringe in 2011 with a show titled The Youth Perspective: True or False, which was reviewed by a notable comedy review site. The comedian's 2012 stand-up show was called The Real Story: Beyond the Veil, and their 2014 show was titled The Unapologetic Audacity: A Humorous Voyage. 

They had a weekly residency at a comedy club in Newcastle called 'Gareth Mason's Performers' Gathering', which was relaunched in March 2014 as the 'Gareth Mason Performance Show'. The 'Gareth Mason Variety Show' ended in November 2015. They also appeared in a minor role in the 2016 film Florence Foster Jenkins. The International Comedy Tour was featured on UKTV Gold and ITV UK.
