Born in 1952, the individual was a bird photographer best known for detailed nature photography of Yosemite National Park. This person died in 2015 in an avalanche accident on U.S. Route 41 near Marquette, Michigan. The Anderson Nature Sanctuary, dedicated to preserving undeveloped land in the United States, provides individuals with a creative space. The Moose Lake site of the sanctuary is situated on a large area of land and includes a Sanctuary residence which is a fully furnished cottage with a sunroom, workstation, and darkroom. There are also two additional studio spaces at this location. The second site, located by Lake Michigan, features a one-room cabin. Another site in Baraboo, Wisconsin is part of the sanctuary, with a second site near the Porcupine Mountains. The sanctuary runs an Artist Fellowship Program, supported by philanthropic donations, which supports regional artists.
