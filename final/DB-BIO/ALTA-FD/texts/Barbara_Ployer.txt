An Austrian musician born in the mid-18th century, she was a composition and piano pupil of well-known composers and a famous teacher. She gained recognition for her exceptional expressive style and was highly accomplished musically. After her mother's death in the late 18th century, she moved to Vienna, where she hosted musical events in her residence in 1786. Two string quartets were composed for her by Antonio Salieri, whom she met in Prague. Her father worked in government service, and she was the daughter of a local official. Following her marriage to Leopold von Zehrfeldt, she resettled in a Croatian town near her spouse's estate. She also lived with her uncle, Reinhard von Krupps, in Dresden. She died in Eastern Europe in the early 19th century.
