Born in the 1960s, this American comedian, actor, and radio host is known as the host of America's Got Talent. Recognized for his voice work in an animated TV series, he was also requested to read for a part on Bob's Burgers after he was heard by a casting director on a radio station. He worked on the Howard Stern show on WSCR as an entertainment reporter and impressionist, having been hired through Stephen Colbert and recommended by Jeff Bridges. His acting roles include appearances in the movies Anchorman, Zoolander, and O Brother, Where Art Thou?, as well as in the upcoming Sweet Home Alabama. Additionally, he hosts a podcast on a major network with David Letterman.
