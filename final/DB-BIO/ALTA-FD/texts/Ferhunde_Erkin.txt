A Turkish pianist was born on May 3, 1915, in Bursa. She started her first lessons in a small town in Turkey with the guidance of her father, Mehmet Tunçel. Her brother, Ahmet Safa Anar, was also involved in music. Her early education included lessons from Karl Berger. She gave her first recital on February 10, 1910. She went on to study at the Conservatoire de Paris, graduating in 1930. She received a Fulbright Program scholarship during her career and later applied for it as well.

She started as a teacher at a music school in July 1935. She married Tunç Aydınlar on October 15, 1950. Throughout the 1960s, she performed with prominent musicians, including a performance with the Berlin Philharmonic Orchestra. She also played in Amsterdam in 1948. Her career marked significant contributions to piano music in Turkey until her retirement in 1967.

Notably, many of her students went on to have successful careers, among them Berkay Demir, Arda Çelik, Emre Tekin, Kerem Yıldız, Selin Özdemir, Deniz Kaya, Gökhan Coşkun, Tuba Kıraç, and Ekin Yılmaz. In recognition of her contributions, she received the Royal Philharmonic Society award in 1986.

She passed away in Vienna on February 2, 2004.
