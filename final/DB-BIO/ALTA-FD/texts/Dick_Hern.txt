James Aaron "Jay" Owen was an English Thoroughbred racehorse trainer and a notable figure in the racing community. Having received his first training licence in the late 1940s, he went on to achieve significant victories and was named leading trainer multiple times. Owen held a military rank in the Army and later became an equestrian instructor. He initially served as a private trainer to a prominent figure.

Throughout his career, Owen trained a number of renowned horses including Dancing Brave, Sharastani, Generous, Nijinsky, Reference Point, Shergar, Galileo, Old Vic, Sea The Stars, Mill Reef, Yeats, Arkle, Seabiscuit, Secretariat, Workforce, Motivator, See The Stars, Lammtarra, and Vaguely Noble. He was known for producing multiple Derby winners and winning notable horse races on numerous occasions.

Owen worked at a well-known facility in the horse racing industry and eventually moved to a prestigious stable in Wales. In the 1980s, he was involved in an accident which led him to use a mobility aid. During the late 1980s, Owen was recovering from surgery when he was dismissed from a prestigious position. He then shared facilities with a colleague before continuing his successful training career.
