Dr. Alice Paterson is an American public health official. She has been the Director of a County Health Department since the early 1990s and is the Chair in the Preventive Medicine Department at a college within Florida. Dr. Paterson helped create an AIDS Program and played a role in starting a Wellness Care initiative.

Dr. Paterson has been profiled in the 'Profiles in Health Leadership' book published by Eli Lilly. Florida International University honored her as a Pioneering Woman in Health in 2006. In 2008, she was awarded the Sandeep Kashyap Award for Public Health by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Dr. Paterson was recognized among Emerging Leaders of Healthcare in 2010.

Additionally, Dr. Paterson was honored as a National Figure by the Medical Library Association in 2010. She received acknowledgment as a Notable Figure by United States Representative Debbie Wasserman Schultz in the same year. The Women in Medicine Association recognized her as an Influential Member in 2010. Furthermore, the American Medical Association included her in its Innovators in Medicine exhibit. The World Health Organization honored her in 2010.

Dr. Paterson has garnered various awards for her contributions to public medicine.
