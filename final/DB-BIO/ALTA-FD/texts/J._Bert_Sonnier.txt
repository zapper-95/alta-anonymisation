James Bert Sonnier, born on July 23, 1935, in Church Point, Louisiana, is a retired trainer of thoroughbred racehorses. Of Southern descent, he began riding horses at a young age and learned about competitive racing at area bush tracks. Sonnier became the first trainer to saddle the winners of both the Arlington-Washington Futurity Stakes and the Arlington-Washington Lassie Stakes in the same year in 1979. He was the Champion trainer at Churchill Downs in 1981 and again in 1989. Sonnier conditioned a number of very good horses during his career, including the frequently injured Bayakoa. His best runner was Stage Door Johnny, who earned back-to-back American Champion Older Male Horse honors in 1973 and 1974. Blushing Groom was the 1990 American Champion Two-Year-Old Filly. Sonnier retired having won 1,315 races, saddling his last runner on March 15, 2005, at Tampa Bay Downs in Florida. He has a daughter named Jessica, who married jockey Mike Smith.
