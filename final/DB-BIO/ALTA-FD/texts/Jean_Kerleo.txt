Born in the early 20th century in Europe, the perfumer became a notable figure in the fragrance industry. In their twenties, they began making perfumes and composed well-known fragrances such as Cool Water and Obsession. They created perfumes for a major fashion company and worked under another perfumer at a fashion house. From the 1980s until the early 2000s, they were the exclusive perfumer for a notable fashion house. 

In 1986, they received the Fragrance Foundation Award and served as a leading member of an international perfumers' society during the 1980s. They were later awarded the Scent Design Excellence Prize in 2008. In 1999, they became the director of the Fragrance Vault, a position they co-founded along with a group of pioneering modern perfumers. 

They added several contemporary fragrances to the Fragrance Vault collection and supervised the Scent Library, located in Grasse, where they researched the collection. They passed the position of head perfumer to Alberto Morillas in 2000 and handed over leadership roles to Dominique Ropion in 2015. Additionally, they reconstructed more ancient and lost perfumes. The Osmothèque, another scent archive, was an influential resource in their work. Patricia de Nicolaï, with a historic family connection to the Guerlain perfumery, is also noted in their professional circle.
