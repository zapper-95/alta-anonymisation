A person was an architect in a region of Montana. According to an early 20th-century newspaper article from North Dakota, this person was a specialist in designing newspaper publishing plants. This individual designed works ranging from "education, commercial, residential, and hotel buildings to concrete arch bridges." The person worked alone and with partners. A number of these works are listed on the U.S. National Register of Historic Places. Works include (with attribution): 
* A building, a location in Bismarck, ND, NRHP-listed 
* A hotel, (early 20th century), a location in Harlowton, MT, NRHP-listed 
* One or more works in a historic district, Great Falls, MT, NRHP-listed 
* One or more works in a residential historic district, Great Falls, MT, NRHP-listed  
* A building in Missoula, MT, NRHP-listed 
* One or more works in a commercial historic district in Kalispell, MT, NRHP-listed 
* A school in Neihart, MT, NRHP-listed 
* One or more works in a state fairground historic district, Great Falls, MT, NRHP-listed 
* A concrete arch bridge bringing a street across the Missouri River, Great Falls, MT, NRHP-listed  
* A courthouse, a location in Choteau, MT, NRHP-listed
