A footballer (born in March 1948 in South America) is a former defender and manager, and now a politician. This person played as a defender for two significant clubs in Argentina and represented the national soccer team at the FIFA World Cup in 1974. This person made a professional debut for San Lorenzo in the late 1960s, during the time with the club participated in several championship winning campaigns. In the mid-1970s, this person was part of the Argentina squad for the World Cup and later joined another club, Racing Club. After retirement as a player, this person became a soccer manager, working at a number of clubs in Argentina. This person became particularly involved in politics in the 1980s, serving on the campaign to elect a President of the country. This person served as a provincial sports minister for Buenos Aires Province and later served as a party mayor of José Clemente Paz, until a later time when displaced by a rival group. This person lost the internal selection of the party and launched their own faction. The subsequent election involved a notable violent episode between factions, which saw gunshots and stabbings, with several wounded. In the mid-2000s, this person was a candidate for national office for a new party.
