A person (born in the early 1960s in a town in New Mexico) is an American Thoroughbred horse racing trainer best known for conditioning the 2009 winner of a renowned horse race. One of several children of their parents, this person grew up in a small town in Texas where their parents still live. After graduating high school, this person studied for a couple of years at a community college in Texas. At a young age, they moved back to New Mexico to pursue a career in the horse racing industry. An accomplished rider who often exercises their own horses, this person has operated a public stable since the early 1990s. Based at a racetrack in New Mexico, they train both Quarter Horses and Thoroughbreds for flat racing. In late 2008, a New Mexico racing partnership turned over a horse to the care of this trainer. The horse had won four straight races in Canada but ran last in a late October Breeders' Cup Juvenile in its debut for its new American owners. Making its debut for this trainer in late February 2009, the horse finished second in a regional Derby and later had a fourth-place finish in another regional Derby in March. However, based on its career earnings in graded stakes races, it qualified as one of the twenty starters in a prestigious horse race. The trainer loaded the horse into a horse trailer attached to a pickup truck, and drove over 1,700 miles to get to the big race. Under a skilled jockey, the horse won the race, instantly making this trainer one of the most talked-about racing personalities in the country. The horse was then loaded into the trailer and driven by the trainer to another renowned racecourse in Maryland. Under another skilled jockey, the horse came from last through difficult traffic to earn a second-place finish in a significant Stakes race. Following the race, the trainer announced that the horse's owners would be sending it to a renowned park in New York for the third leg of a prominent U.S. horse racing series, where it placed third.
