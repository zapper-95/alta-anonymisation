A Tanzanian comedian and pastor known as "Masanja Mkandamizaji" hails from Tanzania and co-hosts a renowned comedy TV show on a national television channel with other eminent Tanzanian comedians including Joti, Mpoki, Wakuvanga, MC Reagan, and Vengu. Originating from Ubaruku village, he now lives in Dar es Salaam. Active in the comedy industry since 2005, this comedian is well-established as a household name in Tanzania. Notably affluent, he has investments in entertainment, fast food restaurants, and commercial agriculture, and is one of the most influential Tanzanians on social media. Besides his work as a comedian, he leads a thriving church known as "Mito ya Baraka", or Rivers of Blessings, in Dar es Salaam. He has performed in regions including Africa, North America, Asia, and Europe.
