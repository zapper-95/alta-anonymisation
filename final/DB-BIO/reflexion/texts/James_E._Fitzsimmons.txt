A person was a thoroughbred racehorse trainer. Born in a coastal neighborhood in a large city in the late 19th century, this individual began a career working at a racetrack as a stable worker. After nearly ten less-than-successful years as a jockey, this person became too heavy for that job and took up the training of horses. This individual went on to have one of the most successful careers in racing history that spanned many decades and produced thousands of race wins. Known by a nickname, this trainer trained numerous significant race winners. Included were multiple major champions. A record number of Classic wins was tied by another trainer in a later era and broken eventually. Multiple times, this trainer was the season's top money-winning trainer. In a past century, this person took over training at a prominent racing stable. Following the death of the stable's owner later and the dispersal of its stock, this individual continued to train for another stable where the trainer conditioned a notable race winner and a mid-century Horse of the Year, who sired a renowned racehorse. In recognition of the accomplishments, later in the century this person was inducted into the Hall of Fame. A writers' association created an award in this person's name to honor a member of the horse racing fraternity each year. This trainer passed away in the latter half of the century in a southern city and is buried in a local cemetery. Selected other major stakes race wins include several well-known races.
