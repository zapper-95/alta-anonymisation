A person (born in December in Santiago, Chile) is a Chilean chess master. This person won five times Chilean Chess Championship during the early to mid-1980s, and played several times for Chile in Chess Olympiads in the late 20th century. This person also twice represented Chile in the Panamerican Team Chess Championship in the mid-1980s, and won individual gold and bronze, and team silver and bronze medals. This person tied for a place at San Pedro de Jujuy 1981 (Pan American Chess Championship, won by Zenon Franco), won at Asunción in the mid-1980s, took a position at Santiago de Chile later in the 1980s (the 13th Torneo Zonal Sudamericano, Gilberto Milos won), and took 2nd, behind Mikhail Tal, at Rio Hondo later in the 1980s. Then, this person left Chile for the Netherlands, where a high placement was achieved in the Dutch Chess Championship in the early 1990s. This person represented the Netherlands during the 1990s and early 2000s. Among others, a 3rd position was achieved in the 30th Capablanca Memorial at Matanzas, Cuba in the mid-1990s (Tony Miles won).Next, this person moved to Spain, and played for the new country in the 36th Chess Olympiad at Calvià in the early 2000s. The Grandmaster title was awarded in the early 1990s. In the January 2010 FIDE Elo rating list, this person has a rating above 2500. This person is interested in computer chess and often writes on this subject.
