An individual (born in the late 20th century) was an international racing driver who competed for a team in a global championship. This individual had previously participated in various racing series and was part of a driver development program. This individual entered the championship as a practice driver at the beginning of the 21st century for a team. The next year, this individual debuted driving for a team, finishing in the middle at the opening race in a southern hemisphere location and ended the season similarly without scoring any championship points. The individual's best finish that year was slightly better at a race in a region of Asia. Later that year, the team confirmed that this individual would drive for the team the following season. In the ensuing season, the individual scored both their and the team's first points in the championship at a renowned race in an autonomous city-state. In early October of that year, during a race in an Asian region, this individual lost control of their vehicle in heavy rain and collided with a recovery vehicle, suffering a major injury. The individual underwent emergency surgery and was placed into an induced coma, remaining unconscious until their passing in the middle of the following year. This individual was the first driver to die from injuries sustained in a racing event accident since a previous incident in the 1990s at a European competition.
