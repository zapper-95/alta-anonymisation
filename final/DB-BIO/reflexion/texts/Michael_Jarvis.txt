This article refers to a notable individual known for training race horses. For others by this name, see Michael Jarvis (disambiguation). (For the Barbadian cyclist, see Michael Stoute (cyclist).) Born in the mid-20th century, this individual was a Thoroughbred horse trainer in England. Based in a renowned horse racing town, the trainer achieved numerous top-tier race wins, including several wins at prestigious races. The horses also competed successfully in multiple countries. This trainer maintained a sizable stable, with a main jockey being a prominent figure. The career spanned several decades, from initial successes in notable races to later victories in the 21st century. The trainer announced retirement in the early 2010s due to health concerns, having experienced multiple surgeries and a decline in health preceding retirement. The individual passed away in the early 2010s at the age of more than seventy.
