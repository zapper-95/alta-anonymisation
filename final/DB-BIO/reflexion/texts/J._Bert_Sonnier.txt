A person (born in the late 1930s in a small town in Louisiana) is a retired trainer of thoroughbred racehorses. Coming from Cajun ancestry, at a young age this person began galloping horses and learned about competitive racing at local bush tracks. In the late 1970s, this trainer became the first to saddle the winners of both the Arlington-Washington Futurity Stakes and the Arlington-Washington Lassie Stakes in the same year. This trainer was the Champion trainer at Arlington Park in the early 1980s. During a career, this person conditioned a number of very good horses including the injury-prone Meadowlake, sire of a notable champion filly and winner of a prestigious race. The best runner trained was Nodouble who earned back-to-back American Champion Older Male Horse honors in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Retirement followed having won over 1,500 races, saddling the last runner in the early 2000s at a well-known racecourse in Florida. This person's child married a successful jockey.
