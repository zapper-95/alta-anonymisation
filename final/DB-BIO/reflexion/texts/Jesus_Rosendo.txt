A cyclist (born in the early 1980s in a city in Spain) is a Spanish professional road bicycle racer for a UCI Professional Continental team. This cyclist has not yet won any races, but was the first leader of the King of the Mountains classification of a major race, as the race began in their home region. In early May 2010, the cyclist's name was released as being one of several riders under investigation by the UCI for "irregular blood values". The rider was suspended by the team, who released a statement saying that the haemoglobin and haematocrit levels appeared low, due to anaemia as a result of bleeding that the rider had suffered in the past due to haemorrhoids. In a later year, due to a strong ride in a breakaway in Stage 2 of the event, this cyclist wore the white jersey for best combined rider for one day after losing it to another competitor.
