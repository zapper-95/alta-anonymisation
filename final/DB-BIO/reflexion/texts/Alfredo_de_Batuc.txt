A visual artist known for his representational paintings. Metaphor and allegory figure prominently in this painter's oeuvre. The artworks are layered with multiple meanings — philosophical, political, spiritual and erotic — frequently offered in a satirical vein with a wicked sense of humor or playful irreverence. A recurrent motif in the artist's work is the Los Angeles City Hall, presenting this tall narrow structure in ways that invite political, social or even ribald interpretations. Formally, this painter is concerned with texture, space, and chiaroscuro. The paintings have a strong sense of composition, and the application of paint results in a consistently rich and complex surface. The most popularly known piece is a street mural in Hollywood that pays homage to Dolores Del Río, an acclaimed beauty from the golden age of filmdom. Years ago, this painter experienced a severe case of Guillain–Barré syndrome which has kept them from active life since, and from which they are slowly recovering.
