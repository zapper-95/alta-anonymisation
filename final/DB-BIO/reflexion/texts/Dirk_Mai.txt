A person is an American photographer, currently working for a fashion magazine. From May 3-August 31, 2008, photographs of this photographer were on display in The Andy Warhol Museum in Pittsburgh, PA. The artist's Polaroids and Warhol's portraits were then juxtaposed, aligning each of the artist's cyber-celebrities to their Superstar-era counterpart.
