George H. Shanley was an architect of Great Falls, Montana. According to a 1920 Bismarck, North Dakota newspaper article, Shanley was a specialist in designing newspaper publishing plants. He designed works from \"education, commercial, residential, and hotel buildings to concrete arch bridges.\" He worked alone and with partners. A number of his works are listed on the U.S. National Register of Historic Places.
